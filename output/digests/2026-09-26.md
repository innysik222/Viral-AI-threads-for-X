More context:

In contrast, Russia's broad objectives and poor decision-making led to significant losses and resource depletion. Opportunity Costs and Resource Allocation Russia's decision to build extensive railway systems for its military was a costly misallocation of resources. These investments diverted funds from addressing the core objectives of the war.
