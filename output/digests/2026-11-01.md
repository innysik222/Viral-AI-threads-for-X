1. Using Agents for Long-Horizon Financial Work — Aman Singh, Arca

What the video argues:
Arca, an AI-native wealth management firm, is transforming financial services by leveraging AI agents to provide proactive and efficient client service. With internal benchmarks guiding model selection and strict safety protocols, Arca ensures reliable and compliant operations.

Key details:
- AI Agents Replace Back-Office Work: At Arca, AI agents handle the bulk of the work around advisors, freeing them to focus on empathetic and human-centric services.
- Proactive Service: Arca's agents operate 24/7, constantly analyzing market conditions and client needs to provide timely and relevant advice.
- Model Agnosticism: Arca uses a model agnostic approach, opting for the best-suited model based on internal benchmarks rather than external validation.

More context:
Proactive Financial Management: Aman Singh, Arca's AI platform lead, explained that their firm's ultimate goal is to run the business with AI. Model Selection: Arca's approach to model selection is unique. Singh mentioned, "We have our own built internally that is model agnostic.
