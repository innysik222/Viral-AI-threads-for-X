3. How Chess.com Became the World’s Biggest Chess Community with CEO Erik Allebest

What the video argues:
Chess.com, once a niche domain purchase by founder CEO Erik Allebest in 2005, has grown into the world's largest chess community. It is now valued at over $200 million annually and boasts more than 10 million daily active users.

Key details:
- Cultural Shift: Chess is experiencing a resurgence due to cultural phenomena like "The Queen’s Gambit" and short-form content.
- AI Innovation: AI is transforming chess, making it both more engaging and challenging for players of all skill levels.
- Bootstrapped Growth: The business grew organically without external funding, highlighting the potential in niche markets with dedicated communities.

More context:
Cultural Shift: A Game of Cultural Icons Erik Allebest credits the cultural resurgence to phenomena like "The Queen’s Gambit" and short-form content platforms. These factors have driven millions of new players into chess, making it a mainstream hobby once again. AI Transformation: Evolving Beyond Checkmate According to Erik, AI is enhancing the game by pushing human players to improve their skills and enjoy the process more deeply. This transformation has made chess accessible to a broader audience while maintaining its competitive integrity. Bootstrapped Success: From Domain Name to Billion-Dollar Empire Chess.com's journey began with a $56,000 domain name purchase from bankruptcy auction in 2005. The company grew organically and became profitable within 18 months of launch, raising over $200 million annually without seeking external funding.
