What the video argues:
Voice AI is entering a phase of hybrid deployments, where cascaded models provide robust control while newer speech-to-speech models offer more natural interactions. Engineers are continuously balancing intelligence, latency, and reliability to meet complex customer needs.

Key details:
- Cascaded Models vs. Speech-to-Speech Models: Cascaded models like those from Pipecat ensure strict guardrails but come with the trade-off of higher complexity.
- Latency and Reliability Trade-offs: Intelligence in responses often comes at the cost of increased latency, making reliability a critical factor in deployment decisions.
- Complexity of Turn-Taking: Voice agents must accurately detect when to interject or wait for human input, requiring sophisticated models.
