What the video argues:
In a conversation with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon, we explore why diffusion models will dominate AI inference, especially in text and code generation, due to their inherent speed and efficiency. Key details:
- Speed and Efficiency: Ermon emphasizes that speed and efficiency will be crucial in the next few years of AI development.
- Diffusion Models: These models are inherently more parallel, making them better suited for inference tasks compared to auto-regressive models.
- Commercial Scale: Inception is betting on diffusion models for commercial-scale language models, aiming to offer faster and more efficient solutions.

More context:
The Future of AI: Speed and Efficiency Stefano Ermon, co-founder and CEO of Inception, highlights the importance of speed and efficiency in the future of AI. According to Ermon, "Inference time scaling and efficiency are going to be the name of the game in AI over the next few years." This shift is driven by the need to process data faster and more efficiently, especially as AI applications become more widespread. The Advantages of Diffusion Models Ermon explains that diffusion models, which are based on iterative denoising, offer a more parallel approach compared to auto-regressive models. "A diffusion model is built to have an inference workload that maps really well to GPUs," he says. This inherent parallelism makes diffusion models more efficient, allowing them to generate text and code much faster than auto-regressive models.
