More context:
Context and the Quadratic Bottleneck Transformer models, while powerful, suffer from a significant inefficiency known as the quadratic bottleneck. As the context length increases, the computational cost grows quadratically. Doubling the context length increases the compute cost by a factor of four. This makes handling large datasets and complex tasks extremely resource-intensive. The Subquadratic Solution Alex Whedon, co-founder and CTO of Subquadratic, has developed a sparse attention mechanism that addresses this issue. Unlike previous approaches, Subquadratic’s solution uses a single model to dynamically select the most important token relationships without the need for a separate selection mechanism.
