# Revolutionizing Enterprise Automation: Serval’s Approach to AI-Driven Code Generation

**TL;DR:** Serval Co-Founder and CEO Jake Stauch is leading the charge in enterprise automation by introducing coding agents that can be used by non-engineers, transforming how enterprises handle their IT processes and automations.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **AI-Driven Code Generation:** Serval empowers IT teams to build internal tools without coding skills, leveraging AI to generate code.
*   **Security and Compliance:** While making automation accessible, Serval maintains strict control and compliance, ensuring safety in large enterprises.
*   **Personal Agents in the Enterprise:** Serval envisions a future where personal agents will handle routine tasks, streamlining workflows and increasing productivity.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

**AI-Driven Code Generation**
Serval Co-Founder Jake Stauch asserts that coding agents are the future of enterprise automation. These agents allow non-technical users to build internal tools and automations by generating code, making the process accessible to a broader audience. "We are the way that a large enterprise with a lot of rules and approvals and process and politics can adopt these tools safely and comfortably at scale," states Stauch.

**Security and Compliance**
While AI-driven code generation enhances accessibility, it also introduces significant security risks. Stauch emphasizes the importance of guardrails and approval processes. "We are the boundary. We are the guardrails," he says. "Servil is the way that a large enterprise can adopt these tools safely and comfortably at scale."

**Personal Agents in the Enterprise**
Serval is also working on personal agents that will be used by employees for routine tasks. "Everyone will have personal agents that do tasks for them," Stauch predicts. These agents will help reduce manual workloads and increase efficiency, while still adhering to strict enterprise policies.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "We are the boundary. We are the guardrails. The intelligence getting better just makes our product more powerful and also makes our customers want to actually clamp down even further on those boundaries on those guardrails." - Jake Stauch

### 🏁 Conclusion
Serval’s approach to enterprise automation is poised to disrupt traditional solutions like ServiceNow. By providing coding agents for non-engineers and maintaining strict security and compliance, Serval is setting a new standard for enterprise automation. As the tech landscape evolves, Serval’s vision of personal agents and streamlined workflows is likely to shape the future of how businesses operate.
