### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### **The Looming Threat of World War III**
Dr. Sarah Paine, a world-renowned expert on the subjects of World War II and current geopolitical tensions, warns that the world is barreling towards a new World War, possibly on the scale of World War II. Paine argues that Putin's actions in Ukraine, characterized by unlimited objectives, could lead to a prolonged and deadly conflict. She states, "The way countries used to organize themselves and often they were empires was by land, but now it's a maritime world order which is about compounding wealth and trade." This shift in power dynamics could exacerbate conflicts and lead to a global catastrophe.

#### **Unlimited Objectives and Pivotal Errors**
Paine explains that unlimited objectives, such as Putin's desire to reclaim the Soviet empire, can transform a conflict into a prolonged and deadly struggle. She refers to the concept of "pivotal errors," such as Putin's invasion of Ukraine, which has contributed to the Ukrainian national identity and made it much harder for Russia to back down. Paine argues, "A pivotal error is once you've made it, the choices that you had before, they're a lot better than ones you have now. And you never get the old ones back." This means that once a country commits to an unlimited objective, the consequences are likely to be severe and long-lasting.

#### **The Collapse of the Rules-Based Order**
The collapse of the rules-based order and the normalization of lies are significant threats to global stability. Paine argues that the Soviet Union's collapse was in large part due to its foundation on lies. She states, "The Soviet Union was a society that collapsed in large part because it was built on lies." The current normalization of lies in the United States and other countries, such as the Iran war and the disinformation campaign, are contributing to the erosion of the rules-based order. Paine warns, "The further back you stop these people, the safer you are."

#### **Economic and Strategic Consequences**
The Iran war and Trump's policies have had significant negative impacts on the US economy and alliance system. Paine argues that the Iran war was incompetently executed, trashing the US economy and blowing up the US alliance system. She states, "This war has trashed the US economy. It has blown the US alliance system, but it also has the collateral damage of taking down the world's economy." The normalization of lies and the collapse of the rules-based order have contributed to these negative consequences.
