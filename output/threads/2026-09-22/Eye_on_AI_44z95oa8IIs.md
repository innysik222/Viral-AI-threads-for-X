### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Early Adoption and Success**
The interview with Major Phoenix, who leads Lazar's Group, provides a detailed insight into how Ukraine has transformed its drone capabilities from mere reconnaissance to lethal weapons. Major Phoenix explains that the first use of drones as weapons occurred in May 22, 2022, when Russian tanks and vehicles were stopped in a convoy. The group managed to destroy these targets using drones, marking a significant shift in their operational strategy.

**Satellite-Linked Drones and Long-Range Missions**
The group's focus on satellite-linked drones has been crucial. These drones can operate over long distances, with Major Phoenix highlighting the ability to fly up to 70 kilometers. He explains, "We focused on satellite links because of a lot of opportunities when we use satellite link. There is no limitation by landscape. We can fly far away low altitude as far as we can." This capability has been instrumental in identifying and destroying enemy targets.

**FPV and Heavy Bombers**
To cover the front line, the group uses a combination of FPV (First Person View) drones for the first 10-20 kilometers and heavy bombers for the next 20-35 kilometers. Major Phoenix notes, "Our main goal is armored vehicle, but maybe one year ago, maybe two years ago, we start using FPV because we understand that using expensive drones to heat enemy personnel, it's it's not good, right?" This strategy ensures a comprehensive coverage of the front line, from personnel to armored vehicles.
