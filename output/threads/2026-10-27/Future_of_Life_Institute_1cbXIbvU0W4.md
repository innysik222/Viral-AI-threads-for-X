## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# The Hidden Forces Behind AI Policy: Who Decides Your Future?

**TL;DR:** The video reveals that while the public perceives AI policy to be driven by elected officials, the real power lies in understanding the "power mapping" of veto points. Alex Borez, a key strategist in passing the RAISE Act, introduces a new organization, Who Decides, to unite coalitions and ensure a pro-human future.

### 🔑 Key Insights
* **Power Mapping:** Elected officials are often held back by unexpected veto points, not just having a champion.
* **Coalition Building:** Passing a bill requires identifying the groups and people with influence and strategically organizing them.
* **Pro-Human Future:** Alex Borez emphasizes the need for a broad coalition to ensure a pro-human approach to AI policy.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Power Mapping
Alex Borez, a seasoned strategist, explains that the key to passing a bill is understanding the "power mapping" of potential veto points. He emphasizes that no one person can say "yes" to turning a bill into a law, but there are probably a baker's dozen who can say "no." He uses the RAISE Act as an example, illustrating how the key to passing a bill is not just having a champion but understanding the various veto points and the incentives of those who can say "no."

#### Coalition Building
The RAISE Act was a significant example of how coalition building can make or break a bill. Borez shares the story of how Y Combinator organized a letter from startups to say that the RAISE Act would be devastating to startups. While the initial letter was effective, the opposition from the former president of Y Combinator and other New York City or State VCs and startups was more effective. The letter stated, "We support this bill, and let's move it forward." This example highlights the importance of a united coalition.

#### Pro-Human Future
Alex Borez introduces a new organization, Who Decides, to unite coalitions and ensure a pro-human approach to AI policy. He emphasizes that while the opposition is actively organizing to prevent meaningful regulation, the pro-human side needs to organize across broad sectors. Borez states, "We must organize across broad sectors to bring everyone together. It’s time for the pro-human side to have an opposite."

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The key to passing a bill is understanding the power mapping of potential veto points, not just having a champion." - Alex Borez

### 🏁 Conclusion
Alex Borez’s introduction of Who Decides marks a significant step in the fight for a pro-human future. The organization aims to unite coalitions and ensure that the American people, not just five CEOs, decide the future of AI policy. The real question is, do you want a future where 5 CEOs decide the future, or do you want the American people to be the ones who decide?
