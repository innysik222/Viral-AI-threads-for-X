# AI’s New Role in Mathematics: The Future is Here

**TL;DR:** The future of mathematics is intertwined with AI, with tools like Lean proving theorems and checking proofs with unprecedented accuracy.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **AI Verification:** Lean and other proof assistants are verifying complex mathematical proofs, ensuring correctness at an unprecedented scale.
*   **Formalization Gap:** Fields Medalists' work has revealed a gap between what is written on paper and what is formally checked, highlighting the need for precise formalization.
*   **Mathematical Engineering:** The process of formalizing proofs is akin to software engineering, emphasizing the need for clear definitions and structured libraries.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### AI Verification in Mathematics
The advent of AI in mathematics through tools like Lean is revolutionizing the field. As Kevin Hartnett explains, "AI is coming for mathematics. This is absolutely clear." Lean, a proof assistant, is not only verifying proofs but also generating new insights. For instance, AlphaProof, a system based on the same architecture as AlphaGo, achieved a silver medal score on the International Mathematical Olympiad, proving theorems and generating proofs.

#### Formalization Gap
Mathematicians like Kevin Buzzard have found significant errors in the work of Fields Medalists, revealing a gap between what is written and what is formally checked. Buzzard notes, "We found we found four Fields medalists have actually proved theorems in the annals of mathematics. And if you actually read the statement of the theorem, then strictly speaking, that theorem is not true." This gap underscores the need for more precise formalization in mathematical proofs.

#### Mathematical Engineering
The formalization of mathematics is increasingly resembling software engineering. Mathematicians are now focusing on clear definitions, APIs, and structured libraries. Johan Commelin and others are creating unit tests for definitions, ensuring that they behave as expected. This process is not just about checking correctness but also about building robust and reusable mathematical tools.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Mathematics should contain no ambiguities at all. So in theory, mathematics is completely well specified and thus perfect for this kind of game, you know, writing it down in a formal system using a computer program." - Kevin Hartnett

### 🏁 Conclusion
As AI tools like Lean continue to evolve, they are transforming the way we approach mathematical proofs. The future of mathematics lies in combining human intuition with the precision of AI. The journey ahead is one of exploration, innovation, and adaptation.
