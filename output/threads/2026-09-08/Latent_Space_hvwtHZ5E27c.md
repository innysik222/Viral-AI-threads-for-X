## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# The Future of Coding: Inside AMP’s Revolution

**TL;DR:** AMP, a 20-person team operating globally, has eliminated mandatory code review and is transforming development practices through the use of AI-driven agents and orbs. This shift is redefining how software is built, run, and used, pushing the boundaries of traditional development practices.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Elimination of Mandatory Code Review:** AMP has embraced AI and trust to streamline the development process, prioritizing speed and quality over bureaucratic review processes.
*   **Orbs and Agents:** These tools have enabled AMP to run development in the cloud, making local dev environments obsolete and dramatically increasing parallelism.
*   **CI is numbered:** AMP's agents can now perform comprehensive testing and automation, making traditional CI systems redundant.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### **AMP's New Approach to Development**

AMP, a 20-person team with members distributed across Europe, the US, and Australia, has revolutionized the way software is developed, run, and used. The team's decision to eliminate mandatory code review and shift to orbs and agents marks a significant departure from traditional development practices.

#### **Elimination of Mandatory Code Review**

Quinn Slack, the leader of the AMP team, asserts that mandatory code review is now dead within the organization. Instead, they rely on AI and trust to ensure quality and accountability. Slack explains, "The most important system is having a team that is really trusted, and AI means that you can have a team of people that are more trusted that have more skin in the game that have more ownership end-to-end." This shift allows the team to focus on delivering value and fixing issues faster, rather than spending time on reviews.

#### **Orbs and Agents**

Orbs are a new way of using AMP, allowing users to run applications in a cloud-based environment. This eliminates the need for local development servers and significantly increases parallelism. Slack notes, "You can shut your laptop, you can do 100 things in parallel, and that way of working has changed the way that we all work on the team." The use of orbs and agents has made development more efficient and collaborative, as team members can work on multiple projects simultaneously without conflicts.

#### **CI is Numbered**

Traditional CI systems are becoming obsolete as AMP's agents can now handle comprehensive testing and automation. Slack states, "CI is just a reproducible environment in which to run your project, and agents can do that much better now." The ability to run tests and fix issues automatically means that CI's deterministic nature is no longer necessary. This shift not only speeds up development but also reduces the overhead associated with maintaining CI pipelines.

### 🏁 Conclusion

AMP's approach to coding represents a significant shift in how software is developed, run, and used. By eliminating mandatory code review, embracing orbs and agents, and redefining CI, the team is pushing the boundaries of traditional development practices. As more companies adopt similar strategies, the future of coding is likely to see a shift towards more efficient, collaborative, and agile development processes.
