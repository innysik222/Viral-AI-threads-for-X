# Catch Psychopaths Hiding In Plain Sight: Expert Insights from Dr Leanne Tenbrinke

**TL;DR:** Dr Leanne Tenbrinke reveals how to spot psychopaths, navigate interactions with dark personalities, and deal with them effectively.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Psychopaths can be spotted in 5 seconds**: They use 'duchen smiles' with crows feet and hand gestures.
*   **Watch for sparse, ambiguous information**: Liars tend to give fewer details, making it harder to verify their stories.
*   **Clear boundaries and assertiveness are crucial**: They target those who lack boundaries, so setting them is important.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### **Psychopathy in 5 Seconds**
Dr Tenbrinke's research shows that psychopaths can be identified quickly by their behavioral cues. Key indicators include:
- **Duchen smiles**: These are genuine-looking smiles with crows feet around the eyes, used to mask true feelings.
- **Hand gestures**: Psychopaths often use excessive hand movements to control the conversation and manipulate others.

#### **Detecting Lies**
Lying is a core trait for psychopaths, and Dr Tenbrinke emphasizes the importance of focusing on:
- **Verifiable details**: Instead of relying on non-verbal cues like eye contact, which can be easily faked.
- **Sparse, ambiguous information**: Liars tend to provide fewer details, making it harder to verify their stories.

#### **Managing Interactions**
Navigating interactions with dark personalities requires:
- **Establishing clear boundaries**: Set explicit rules and stick to them to prevent selfish behavior.
- **Avoid giving power**: Do not allow those with dark traits to have power over others, as it often leads to stress and conflict.
- **Emphasize common identity**: Find shared experiences or interests to build a foundation for cooperation.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "People with high levels of psychopathy tend to have less active and less connected prefrontal cortex, associated with impulse control and moral reasoning." - Dr Leanne Tenbrinke

### 🏁 Conclusion
Understanding and managing interactions with psychopaths is crucial for personal and professional success. By recognizing their cues, setting clear boundaries, and using effective communication strategies, we can navigate these challenging interactions more effectively.
