# The Great American Open Model: ReflectionAI's Beam Pioneers the Future of AI

**TL;DR:** Misha Laskin, CEO of ReflectionAI, discusses the rise of open-source models and the groundbreaking Beam, a 500 billion parameter model that could redefine the future of intelligence.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Open Models vs. Closed Models:** Misha argues that open models are becoming the norm, with 70% of token demand now going to open models.
*   **Beam's Efficiency:** Beam is 3 to 4 times more efficient than similar models, making it a game-changer in the field.
*   **Reinforcement Learning:** Misha sees reinforcement learning as the path to AGI, emphasizing the importance of pre-training and RL in building models.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Shift to Open Models
Misha Laskin of ReflectionAI is leading the charge in the open-source model movement. According to Laskin, the shift from closed models to open models is accelerating. "The state of the world today is that we have a few hundred safety researchers within closed labs that understand how these things work," he says. "Despite their best intentions, it is impossible to cover the long-tail of unintended consequences that these systems might have." This has led to a significant shift in the market, with 70% of token demand now going to open models.

#### Beam: The First Open-Source Model
Beam, ReflectionAI's first open model, is a 500 billion parameter model that is 3 to 4 times more efficient than similar models. This efficiency is a result of both a strong pre-training base and reinforcement learning at the largest scale. "The most challenging part of building open models is talent, compute, and scale of data," Laskin acknowledges. "But it's worth it for the future."

#### Reinforcement Learning: The Path to AGI
Misha believes that the path to AGI is through reinforcement learning. "Reinforcement learning was a little over 10,000 GB300s for four weeks," he explains. "Reinforcement learning jobs are, you know, more complex in the sense that you do both a lot of inference at scale with all sorts of sandboxes for agents and training." This complexity has led to significant advancements in the field, with models like Beam pushing the boundaries of what's possible.

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of AI is open-source, and Misha Laskin is leading the charge. With Beam, ReflectionAI is setting a new standard for efficiency and innovation. The path to AGI is through reinforcement learning, and the open-source model is the future.
