## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Revolutionizing Real-World Services with Netic’s Autonomous Enterprise Vision

**TL;DR:** Netic, founded by Melissa Tokmak, is transforming essential real-world services like HVAC and pet care through AI-driven automation. The company aims to handle every aspect of these businesses except for the human labor component.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **AI-Driven Autonomy**: Netic works with large enterprises in various essential services such as HVAC, plumbing, automotive, and consumer wellness.
*   **Customer-Centric Operations**: AI agents handle customer interactions, optimizing service dispatch based on complex operational rules.
*   **Value Creation for Private Equity Firms**: Tokmak emphasizes the shift from cost-cutting to value-creation with AI in private equity-owned businesses.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Transforming Essential Services

Netic is revolutionizing essential services by integrating advanced AI technologies. Melissa Tokmak, Netic’s founder and CEO, highlights how they work with large enterprises ranging from HVAC providers to pet care services. The company's core mission is to create an autonomous enterprise environment where every aspect of these businesses can be managed efficiently through AI, except for the human labor component.

#### Customer-Centric Operations

One of the key aspects of Netic’s operations is the use of AI agents to handle customer interactions. Melissa explains, "Imagine a billion dollar revenue home services company like HVAC or plumbing. When a customer calls, it's our AI agents that talk to them, understand their needs, and match those with the operational rules of the business." This process involves complex decision-making based on factors such as weather conditions, historical data, and customer lifetime value.

#### Value Creation for Private Equity

Tokmak also discusses how private equity firms are shifting their strategies in response to AI technology. "Private equity companies used to focus on finding gems and cutting costs," she says. "Now, they’re more interested in creating tangible value with these businesses through the use of AI." This shift includes optimizing customer interactions, generating new revenue streams, and enhancing overall operational efficiency.

#### The Future Vision

Melissa envisions a future where every single aspect of a business can be handled autonomously by AI systems. "We want to make sure that companies can fully focus on delivering exceptional service and creating customer delight," she states. This includes integrating AI into various aspects of the business, from data analytics to delivery services.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Imagine a world where your HVAC provider is run by AI." — Melissa Tokmak

### 🏁 Conclusion
Melissa Tokmak’s vision for Netic and the broader application of AI in real-world services highlights the transformative potential of technology. As businesses continue to adapt to this new landscape, companies like Netic are at the forefront of creating autonomous enterprises that can optimize operations and enhance customer experiences.
