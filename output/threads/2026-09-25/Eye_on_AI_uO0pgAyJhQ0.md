# Autonomous Drone Warfare: The Future is Here

**TL;DR:** The technology for fully autonomous attack drones is closer than ever, and NORDA Dynamics is pushing the boundaries of AI in drone operations. The CTO, Alex Liannyi, shares insights on the current state and future potential of drone technology.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   Autonomous targeting and last-mile navigation are already implemented.
*   Human approval is required for drone attacks.
*   Collaboration with drone manufacturers is key.
*   Technology is close to moving the human earlier in the process to the launch of the drone.
*   Improved optics can extend drone guidance range.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### Current State of Drone Technology
Alex Liannyi, CTO of NORDA Dynamics, explains that their last-mile targeting is based on classical algorithms, not advanced AI. "Artificial intelligence is a broad term, and in our case, it's mostly about math and control theory," he states. However, they are working on developing drone systems that can search for and recognize targets autonomously. The modules they use, such as the Raspberry Pi, are efficient and perform well in real-time despite their modest hardware.

#### Human Approval and Safety
Liannyi emphasizes the importance of human oversight in the decision-making process. "Drone does not take any action without pilot approval," he asserts. "We hardcode this condition that the operator needs to select the target." The technology is close to moving the human earlier in the process to the launch of the drone, but Liannyi believes that complete autonomy is not yet necessary.

#### Range and Performance
The drones used by NORDA Dynamics can operate for up to 80 km with a higher payload. Thermal guidance can extend up to 2.5 km with the right optics. Liannyi explains, "We can do better. By using gyro-stabilized cameras, we can increase the distance."

#### Collaboration with Drone Manufacturers
NORDA Dynamics collaborates with hundreds of drone manufacturers in Ukraine to focus on research and development. "We supply modules or software, but we don't build the drones," Liannyi states. This collaboration allows them to focus on the technological aspects while manufacturers handle the production.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Technology is very close to moving the human earlier in the process to the launch of the drone. I'm not sure if it's going to be an efficient way to use the drone, but overall, the technology is very close." - Alex Liannyi, CTO, NORDA Dynamics

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of drone warfare is rapidly approaching, and NORDA Dynamics is at the forefront of this technological revolution. While the technology is advancing, human oversight remains crucial. As the debate over autonomous warfare continues, the capabilities of drones will only continue to grow, making it essential for all stakeholders to stay informed.
