## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Why Enterprise AI Isn’t Delivering ROI: Insights from Ahmed Bashir, DevRev

**TL;DR:** Enterprise AI is falling short of expectations due to a critical gap between sophisticated models and inadequate data management infrastructure. Ahmed Bashir, CEO of DevRev, explains the need for a "shared memory" layer to bridge this gap.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Complexity of Enterprise AI:** Enterprises face organizational complexity, with multiple tools, permissions, and roles, making it challenging to leverage AI effectively.
*   **Shared Memory Layer:** Bashir introduces the concept of a "shared memory" layer, crucial for managing and integrating data across various tools and systems.
*   **Redigitization:** Redigitization is compared to reformatting music, emphasizing the need for a new, AI-native data format to unlock AI's full potential.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Reality of Enterprise AI
Ahmed Bashir, CEO of DevRev, highlights the gap between the hype and reality of enterprise AI. While AI demos work impressively, real-world implementations often fall short due to the complexity of enterprise environments. According to Bashir, "While everybody claimed to have industry-leading AI, CIOs were betting heavily on AI, and very few were actually unlocking real ROI."

#### The Importance of Shared Memory
Bashir introduces the concept of a "shared memory" layer, which is essential for managing and integrating data across various tools and systems. He explains, "While a more sophisticated model cannot always make up for not having a sophisticated memory layer. Swapping in a smarter model didn't win you that much; what mattered was the plumbing of the system." This means that without proper data management, even the most advanced AI models will struggle to deliver meaningful results.

#### Redigitization and Data Management
The conversation also touches on the concept of "redigitization," which Bashir compares to the transition from CDs to Spotify. Just as digital music had to be rethought to support new scenarios and devices, data needs a new, AI-native format to be effective. Bashir notes, "We need yet another data format that is AI native. Something that can structure and index and join and relate and negate information so that it can provide really high value search results, answers, and of course take the right actions."

#### The Flagship Product: Computer
DevRev's flagship product, Computer, is designed to address these challenges. It builds a knowledge graph to connect and manage data across tools, providing a more holistic approach to enterprise AI. As Bashir explains, "Computer's aim is to give you the absolute highest degree of precision, efficiency, and safety. It's team first, multi-model, multi-surface, and collaborative."

#### The Impact on CIOs
CIOs are investing heavily in AI, but the reality is that only a small percentage are seeing the expected return on investment. Bashir attributes this to the lack of governance and orchestration. He states, "CIOs are saying that only 28% of them saw the value that they were expecting. And so absolutely there's a huge percentage of people that were disappointed in the final result."

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "While a more sophisticated model cannot always make up for not having a sophisticated memory layer. Swapping in a smarter model didn't win you that much; what mattered was the plumbing of the system."
> — Ahmed Bashir, DevRev

### 🏁 Conclusion
For true AI success, enterprises must rethink their data architecture. The shared memory layer is not just about models; it's about the plumbing. As Bashir notes, "We need to move from a world where data is put on a platter to a world where data is found, validated, joined, structured, understood, and then used to answer questions or take actions."
