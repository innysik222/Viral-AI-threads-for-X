# Are You Planning 20,000 AI Agents? Capital One’s Warning on Scaling Enterprise AI

**TL;DR:** Capital One, a leader in AI-driven banking, warns that scaling AI in a regulated environment requires balancing innovation with governance. Key insights include the importance of a centralized platform, the need for robust data ecosystems, and prioritizing long-term design.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Centralized Platform:** Capital One built a centralized platform to manage and scale AI across the company, supporting thousands of users and 100 million customers.
*   **Robust Data Ecosystem:** The company focused on building a solid data ecosystem and a platform mindset to connect various AI applications.
*   **Governance and Risk Management:** Capital One prioritizes governance and risk management, ensuring that AI applications are managed and deployed in a well-controlled manner.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Centralized Platform
Capital One, a tech-savvy financial institution, has built a centralized platform to manage and scale AI across the company. The platform is designed to handle thousands of users and 100 million customers. According to Abhijit Bose, who leads the AI ML platform engineering org called MLX at Capital One, the platform includes several layers: orchestration, authoring, and deployment. This structure allows for efficient and scalable deployment of AI applications.

#### Robust Data Ecosystem
Building a robust data ecosystem is crucial for successful AI deployment. Bose emphasizes the importance of data unification, feature stores, and governance. The company has integrated open-source capabilities and built proprietary models to ensure data privacy and security. This approach helps in managing and scaling AI applications across the company.

#### Governance and Risk Management
In a regulated environment, governance and risk management are non-negotiable. Bose explains that the company's approach involves breaking down complex tasks into smaller, specialized tasks and using a validation framework called Macau. This framework ensures that AI applications are safe and accurate, reducing the risk of data leakage or unauthorized data sharing.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The fundamental problem statement is that how can we innovate at the frontier of AI, but at the speed of well-managed and that's really you know, a big focus of our team." - Abhijit Bose

### 🏁 Conclusion
Capital One's experience in scaling AI in a regulated environment offers valuable lessons for other enterprises. Prioritizing long-term design, building a robust data ecosystem, and focusing on governance and risk management are key to successful AI deployment. For those planning to deploy 20,000 AI agents, taking a page from Capital One’s playbook is essential.
