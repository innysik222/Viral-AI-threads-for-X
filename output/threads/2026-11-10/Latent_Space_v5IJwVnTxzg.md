# AI's New Frontier: How Handshake AI is Shaping the Future of Data-Driven AI

**TL;DR:** Handshake AI is at the forefront of creating high-quality, expert-driven data sets for AI, transforming how AI models are trained and evaluated in complex, real-world tasks.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Expert-Driven Data:** Data labeling has evolved from simple tasks like drawing boxes to complex, expert-driven tasks like authoring simulated environments.
*   **Hybrid Process:** Combining expert knowledge with AI models requires a hybrid process where experts iteratively author and refine data.
*   **Complex Tasks:** The complexity of tasks, such as financial modeling or life sciences research, necessitates high-quality, realistic data.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Expert-Driven Data:**  
Handshake AI is revolutionizing the way AI models are trained by focusing on high-quality, expert-driven data. Garrett Nishan Shah explains that data labeling has moved from simple, low-skilled tasks to complex, high-skilled tasks requiring domain expertise. This shift highlights the need for more specialized data teams to ensure models are trained accurately.

**Hybrid Process:**  
The process of creating data is a hybrid of expert authoring and AI evaluation. Experts must author environments, define tasks, and write rubrics to codify what good looks like. This iterative process ensures that the data is both realistic and aligned with real-world tasks. Shah notes, "The shape of data and what these people have to do is just on the polar opposite end of what data labeling was."

**Complex Tasks:**  
Complex tasks, such as financial modeling or life sciences research, require highly realistic and diverse data sets. These tasks often involve multiple steps and require a deep understanding of the domain. Shah states, "To be able to codify expertise in a way that allows you to do automated evals or large-scale RL runs is a really complex and challenging task."

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The core change has been like what is the task? These people aren't like drawing boxes around cars, you know, it's like really really complex stuff." - Garrett Nishan Shah

### 🏁 Conclusion
Handshake AI's approach to creating high-quality, expert-driven data sets is reshaping the future of AI. By combining expert knowledge with advanced AI models, Handshake is ensuring that AI applications in complex, real-world tasks are both accurate and reliable. As AI continues to evolve, the role of experts in training and evaluating models will become increasingly critical.
