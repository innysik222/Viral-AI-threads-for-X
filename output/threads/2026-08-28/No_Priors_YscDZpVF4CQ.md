## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Revolutionizing Data Infrastructure in the AI Era with Eon Co-Founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein

**TL;DR:** Companies are increasingly valuing data over physical assets, as illustrated by Google's $10M purchase of Spirit Airlines' data. The future requires a new approach to data management that balances security, accessibility, and AI integration.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Data is Valuable:** Companies now recognize their data as the most valuable asset.
*   **Non-Human Threats:** New threats from AI agents pose challenges for traditional cybersecurity practices.
*   **Rapid Data Transformation:** Legacy infrastructure must adapt to support modern AI applications and real-time data access.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**The Shift in Valuation:**
In the past, physical assets were seen as a company's most valuable possession. However, recent events like Google’s acquisition of Spirit Airlines' data for $10M highlight that data is now considered more critical than ever before. This shift has driven companies to rethink their data strategies and ensure they can leverage their existing data effectively.

**New Threats in the Enterprise:**
The co-founders of Eon, Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein, emphasize the growing threat from non-human actors—AI agents that have legitimate access but may misuse sensitive data. While traditional security measures focused on human threats are still relevant, they must now be supplemented with methods to detect and prevent unauthorized activity by AI agents.

**Rapid Data Transformation:**
Historical data infrastructure is no longer sufficient for modern enterprise needs. Companies need new tools to efficiently store, process, and access their data in real-time while ensuring security and compliance. Eon has developed a cloud-based platform that maps, classifies, and secures data, making it accessible for AI applications.

**Embracing the New Normal:**
The future of data management lies in embracing the new normal of AI-driven data processing. Companies must not only store and protect their data but also leverage it to create value through advanced analytics and machine learning models. Eon provides solutions that help organizations do just that, enabling them to build a new foundation for data-driven decision-making.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "If you're a CEO today, you constantly get questions about selling your data." - **Eon Co-Founder Gonen Stein**

### 🏁 Conclusion
As the world moves into the AI era, companies must adapt their data infrastructure to meet new challenges and opportunities. Embracing the shift in data valuation, understanding the threats posed by non-human actors, and leveraging modern tools will be crucial for maintaining a competitive edge.
