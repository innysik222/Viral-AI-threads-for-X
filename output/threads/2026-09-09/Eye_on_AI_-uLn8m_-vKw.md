# 86% of Coding Agents' Steps Are Just Reading—Not Solving: Subquadratic's Game-Changing Approach

**TL;DR:** Coding agents spend 86% of their time reading and engineering context, not solving problems. Subquadratic's Alex Whedon and his team have developed a breakthrough sparse attention mechanism that reduces this inefficiency by 40x, paving the way for more intelligent and cost-effective coding agents.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **86% of Coding Agent Steps Involve Context Engineering:** This finding reveals a significant inefficiency in current AI systems.
*   **Subquadratic's Sparse Attention Mechanism:** A revolutionary approach that reduces the compute cost of attention by 40x.
*   **Impact on Enterprise Workflows:** Enterprises can now handle larger and more complex context windows without increasing costs.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### Context and the Quadratic Bottleneck
Transformer models, while powerful, suffer from a significant inefficiency known as the quadratic bottleneck. As the context length increases, the computational cost grows quadratically. Doubling the context length increases the compute cost by a factor of four. This makes handling large datasets and complex tasks extremely resource-intensive.

#### The Subquadratic Solution
Alex Whedon, co-founder and CTO of Subquadratic, has developed a sparse attention mechanism that addresses this issue. Unlike previous approaches, Subquadratic’s solution uses a single model to dynamically select the most important token relationships without the need for a separate selection mechanism. This results in a 40x reduction in compute cost and a 64x reduction in overall compute requirements.

#### Practical Implications
Subquadratic's approach is particularly transformative for enterprise workflows. Currently, many companies are limited by a 100k token context window, which restricts their ability to handle large, complex datasets. With Subquadratic's technology, enterprises can now process much larger datasets, reducing the need for extensive context engineering and significantly lowering the overall cost.

#### Future Directions
Whedon envisions a future where coding agents can read and process entire codebases at once, making them more powerful and efficient. This technology could revolutionize the way AI is used in various industries, from finance to robotics.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "86% of the steps were actually read steps just trying to do that context engineering before the execution review which was only the last 14%." — Alex Whedon

### 🏁 Conclusion
Subquadratic's breakthrough in sparse attention is poised to transform the landscape of AI, making coding agents more intelligent and cost-effective. Enterprises that embrace this technology will see significant improvements in their workflows and capabilities.
