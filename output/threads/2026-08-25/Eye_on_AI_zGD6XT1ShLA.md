## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# The Surprising Truth Behind the Failure of 95% of AI Projects | Manoj Saxena, TrustWise

**TL;DR:** 95% of AI agent projects fail to reach production due to a lack of robust governance and control mechanisms. CEO Manoj Saxena discusses the critical need for new infrastructure to manage these digital workers effectively.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Failure Rate:** 95% of AI projects fail to move from pilots to production.
*   **Governance & Control:** Focus shifts from model accuracy to alignment and runtime governance.
*   **TrustWise Platform:** Offers Guardian and Genesis agents, akin to HR for digital workers.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Failure Rate of 95%
Manoj Saxena, CEO of TrustWise, highlighted that a staggering 95% of AI agent projects fail to reach production. This statistic raises critical questions about the current state of enterprise AI and its deployment challenges. According to Saxena, the issue lies not just in model accuracy but in ensuring these models align with business goals and are governed effectively.

#### The Shift from Model Accuracy to Alignment
Saxena emphasized that while building smarter and bigger models is a priority, there's a critical gap in managing these models once they're deployed. He explains: "Trust has become an issue. It's not simply trusting that the model is accurate, but trusting that it will do what you want it to do and not what it wants to do."

#### TrustWise Platform
Saxena introduced TrustWise’s platform, which includes Guardian agents and Genesis agents. These agents serve as a control layer for AI systems, ensuring they behave according to business policies and guidelines. Saxena likens this to having an HR department for digital workers: "We call it the AI Control Tower, almost like the HR and finance department for hundreds of thousands of agents from multiple vendors."

#### New Technologies on Horizon
The conversation also touched on emerging technologies such as Mythos and OpenCLAW. Saxena noted that while these systems are incredibly powerful, they require robust governance to ensure safe and effective deployment. He mentioned potential cost savings through better control: "We have demonstrated an 83% reduction in costs by improving safety and latency."

#### The Urgent Need for New Infrastructure
Saxena highlighted the importance of new infrastructure to manage AI agents at scale. He stressed that as AI moves from generating output to taking actions, businesses need a way to continuously enforce policies during runtime: "The architecture has now become the risk surface... trust becomes like the silver thread that runs through your entire stack."

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "You can't write up an agent very soon, but risk and compliance need to make sure it is following all internal and external regulations. Security needs to make sure it is safe." — Manoj Saxena

### 🏁 Conclusion
The failure rate of 95% in AI projects underscores the urgent need for robust governance and control mechanisms. TrustWise's innovative platform, with its Guardian and Genesis agents, offers a solution that can help businesses unlock the potential of AI while mitigating risks.
