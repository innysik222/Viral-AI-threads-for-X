### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Ethical Shift in Defense
Yaroslav Azhnyuk, a Ukrainian entrepreneur, discusses the ethical concerns surrounding the use of non-AI weapons. He predicts that within the next decade, it will be considered unethical to use weapons without AI. "The same way as it might become unethical to use cars without AI," Azhnyuk notes. This shift is driven by the need to reduce human error and minimize collateral damage. Azhnyuk explains, "We started with making cameras for pets and now we're making cameras for drones, and we were throwing treats to dogs and now we're throwing explosives to people who came to occupy our land." This transition underscores the rapid evolution of technology and its impact on warfare.

#### Ukraine's Defense Tech Revolution
Ukraine has emerged as a global leader in defense technology, particularly in the development of low-cost, autonomous drones. The country has produced 4 million FPV drones last year, each costing $500. These drones are a stark contrast to the US, which struggles to produce such quantities due to lack of capacity. Ukraine's success in this domain highlights the potential for smaller, agile nations to outpace larger, more bureaucratic counterparts. Eric Smith, another key player in the defense tech industry, is known for his full vertical integration strategy, while Azhnyuk advocates for a modular approach. This competition is shaping the future of defense technology and highlighting the importance of innovation and flexibility.

#### AGI and the Future of AI in Warfare
The conversation around AI in warfare extends beyond just autonomous drones and into the realm of artificial general intelligence (AGI). Azhnyuk discusses the ethical implications of fully autonomous weapons and the need for rapid technological advancement. He argues that AI will not be as dramatic as the Skynet scenario, but rather more akin to robots in factories. "AI-enabled defense systems will be no closer to a Skynet appraising that than a warehouse of 155 caliber shells," he notes. This perspective challenges the notion that AI will lead to an existential threat and instead emphasizes the practical applications of AI in enhancing defense capabilities.
