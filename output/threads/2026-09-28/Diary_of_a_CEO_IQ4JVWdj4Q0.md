# WeWork Founder Adam Neumann's Turbulent Journey: Lessons in Success and Failure

**TL;DR:** Adam Neumann, founder of WeWork, shares his rise and fall, offering valuable insights on leadership, personal growth, and the importance of staying true to one's mission.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Mission Over Money:** Neumann stresses that businesses should focus on their mission, not just financial gain.
*   **Ego Management:** The founder warns about the dangers of letting ego take over, as it can cloud judgment and lead to poor decisions.
*   **Personal Growth:** Neumann advocates for continuous self-reflection and personal development to find one's true calling.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### **Mission Over Money**
Adam Neumann, the former CEO of WeWork, emphasizes that businesses should prioritize their mission over financial gains. In his interview, he discusses how WeWork’s rapid growth led him to prioritize financial success over the company’s core mission of creating a community where people can work and live. This shift in focus ultimately contributed to the company’s downfall.

#### **Ego Management**
Neumann also highlights the importance of managing ego, noting that it can be both a strength and a weakness. He shares the story of how he became overwhelmed by his own ego, leading to poor decision-making and the eventual collapse of the company. He advises leaders to be mindful of their ego and to ensure that it does not cloud their judgment.

#### **Personal Growth**
The founder also emphasizes the importance of personal growth and self-reflection. He encourages individuals to take time to understand their true calling and to work on one aspect of themselves at a time. Neumann believes that by focusing on personal growth, one can find a deeper sense of purpose and fulfillment.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Ego and desire are the same word. Big ego just means big desire. Having huge desire is important to build huge things. But you have to manage it and control it."  
> *— Adam Neumann*

### 🏁 Conclusion
Neumann’s journey offers valuable lessons for aspiring entrepreneurs and business leaders. By staying true to one's mission, managing ego, and prioritizing personal growth, individuals can navigate the complexities of business and leadership more effectively. Neumann’s story serves as a cautionary tale, highlighting the importance of maintaining a balanced perspective and focusing on the long-term goals.
