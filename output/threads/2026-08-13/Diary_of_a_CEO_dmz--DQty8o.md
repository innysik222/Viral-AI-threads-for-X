*   **Environmental Factors:** Noise, light exposure, and the presence of other beings affect your ability to rest.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Silent Killer: Sleep Apnea
Dr. Andy Galpin, a renowned expert in human performance science, reveals that 70-80% of people with clinical sleep apnea remain undiagnosed. This condition, characterized by breathing pauses during sleep, is highly associated with long-term health risks such as cognitive function, mood regulation, and metabolic health.

#### Simple Solutions for Better Sleep
Dr. Galpin advises simple yet effective methods to improve your sleep quality:
- **Kiwis Before Bed:** Consuming two to three kiwis before bed can help you sleep better.
- **Sound Matters:** Consistent noise is more disruptive than occasional loudness, suggesting the use of sound machines at low volumes.

#### Environmental Considerations
Dr. Galpin emphasizes the importance of your sleeping environment:
- **Light Exposure:** Light exposure during the day and in the evening significantly impacts your ability to sleep well.
- **Presence of Others:** Sleeping with partners can be disruptive; consider separate beds or sheets to avoid disturbances.

### 🏁 Conclusion
Dr. Galpin's insights offer practical steps for improving your sleep hygiene and overall well-being. By recognizing the signs and making simple changes, you can enhance your quality of life and avoid the serious health risks associated with undiagnosed sleep apnea.
