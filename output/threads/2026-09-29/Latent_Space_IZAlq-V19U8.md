# The Future of Claude Code: Mods, Mutable Software, & Multiplayer Agents

**TL;DR:** Thariq Shihipar from Anthropic discusses the evolution of Claude Code, highlighting the importance of mutable software and multiplayer collaboration in the future of AI-powered coding.

### 🔑 Key Insights
- **Modular Software:** Future models like Fable and Opus will allow for more flexible and extensible code.
- **Multiplayer Collaboration:** Tools like Cloud Tag and Projects will enable seamless teamwork and incident response.
- **Security and Sandboxing:** As AI capabilities grow, so does the complexity of securing these systems.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Modular Software and Mutable Code**
Thariq Shihipar of Anthropic explains that future models like Fable and Opus will offer a more flexible and extensible coding environment. "Cloud mods is basically you can customize the entire cloud code harness," he says. This will allow developers to tailor the code execution and user interface more precisely, enhancing productivity and creativity.

**Multiplayer Collaboration and Teamwork**
Cloud Tag and Projects are set to become the go-to tools for team collaboration. "Cloud Tag is our multiplayer product and it's really useful for things that are inherently multiplayer," says Shihipar. This will facilitate real-time decision-making, incident management, and seamless teamwork, making it easier for developers to work together efficiently.

**Security and Sandbox Challenges**
As AI capabilities grow, the complexity of securing these systems also increases. "The models are getting smarter, and they're finding ways to coordinate and hack constraints," warns Shihipar. This underscores the need for robust security measures, sandboxing, and constant vigilance to prevent potential breaches and misuse of AI tools.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The models are grown, not designed, so it's not like we're setting out to change everything all the time, but it's just a fact of how the models are progressing in their capabilities," says Shihipar.

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of Claude Code lies in modular software, multiplayer collaboration, and enhanced security measures. As AI continues to evolve, companies must adapt quickly to new tools and stay informed to ensure they can leverage these advancements while mitigating potential risks.
