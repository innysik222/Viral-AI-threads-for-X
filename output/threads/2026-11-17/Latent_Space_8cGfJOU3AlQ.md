# The Future of Visual Intelligence: Black Forest Labs’ Journey

**TL;DR:** Black Forest Labs, co-founded by Andreas Blattmann and Dustin Podell, is revolutionizing AI through their Flux models, which combine image and video generation with advanced robotics.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   Black Forest Labs was born from the success of Stable Diffusion, which changed the trajectory of AI for both founders.
*   The company's latest model, Flux 3, aims to integrate multiple modalities, including images, videos, and actions, to create a more general form of intelligence.
*   Flux Context, a single model for image editing, showcases the potential for highly controllable and efficient AI workflows.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Founders' Journey and Inspiration**
Black Forest Labs was founded by Andreas Blattmann and Dustin Podell, who were inspired by the launch of Stable Diffusion. Blattmann, a co-inventor of latent diffusion, quit his job to focus entirely on AI. Podell, having worked in the open-source community, eventually joined stability and later Black Forest Labs. Their shared vision led to the creation of SDXL and other groundbreaking models.

**Flux 3 and Visual Intelligence**
Flux 3 is a central component of Black Forest Labs' strategy. It aims to create a model that can handle multiple modalities, including images, videos, and actions. This multimodal approach is designed to unlock the full potential of visual intelligence, enabling more complex and dynamic AI applications. According to Podell, "Flux 3 is where we saw this going for a long time. It's about combining all natural representations."

**Flux Context: A Game-Changer for Image Editing**
Flux Context is a standout model within Black Forest Labs. It allows for highly controllable image editing with minimal training, demonstrating the potential for efficient and versatile AI workflows. As Blattmann notes, "Flux Context is a single model for image editing with minimal training, just like 6 hours for clear results."
