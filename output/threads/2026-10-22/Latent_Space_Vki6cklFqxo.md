# The Future of Engineering: How 10X is Revolutionizing the Industry

**TL;DR:** 10X, an AI engineering firm, is transforming how engineers are paid and working, emphasizing output over time. They are also driving standardization and automation in their workflows.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Output-Based Pay:** Engineers at 10X can earn millions based on their output, with a 4x difference in earning capacity.
*   **AI Models and Tools:** Engineers use AI models like Opus and Ant to boost productivity, with Opus 5.5 being the most preferred.
*   **Standardized Workflows:** 10X is pushing for more standardization in engineering workflows, with agents handling 90% of the work.
*   **AI-Driven Transformations:** 10X is using AI to transform mid-tier SaaS solutions, saving companies significant costs.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Output-Based Pay:**
At 10X, engineers are paid based on their output rather than the time they spend working. This model has led to a wide dispersion in earning capacity, with some engineers making millions of dollars a year while others make significantly less. The firm believes that AI is a leverage bet on a human's intellect and work ethic, leading to a higher variance in earning potential.

**AI Models and Tools:**
10X engineers use AI models such as Opus and Ant to boost their productivity. Opus 5.5 is currently the preferred model, with its fast speed and cost-effectiveness. The firm is also exploring the use of other models like Codex for specific tasks, but Opus remains the primary tool.

**Standardized Workflows:**
To standardize their workflows, 10X has created a meta harness and system that helps engineers find the bleeding edge of what AI models can do. This system provides tools to augment their work, allowing them to focus on higher-value tasks. Additionally, 10X is working on a platform to deploy agents into production, which can handle 90% of the work, reducing the need for manual intervention.

**AI-Driven Transformations:**
10X is leveraging AI to transform mid-tier SaaS solutions. They recently replaced a mid-tier SaaS product, saving a company $39,000 per year. This transformation not only saves costs but also provides a more tailored and efficient solution for the company.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The thesis is that AI is really just a leverage bet on a human's intellect and work ethic. And so you're just going to have more variance than ever before in earning capacity." - Alex Lieberman

### 🏁 Conclusion
10X is at the forefront of transforming the engineering industry. By focusing on output-based pay and standardizing workflows, they are driving innovation and efficiency. As AI continues to evolve, 10X is well-positioned to lead the way in the future of engineering.
