### 🧠 Deep Dive
**What is Context Engineering?**
Context engineering is a method that involves designing and building systems to provide relevant information to AI agents at the right moment. This technique ensures that agents remain useful and relevant, avoiding "context rot." The course from deeplearning.ai and Chroma offers a comprehensive approach to mastering this skill.

**Key Techniques in the Course**
The course covers several techniques to improve the performance of AI agents:
- **Multiple Search Tools**: Using various search methods to ensure agents access the most relevant information.
- **Context Management**: Techniques to keep the context useful and relevant by trimming duplicate or irrelevant text.
- **Hands-On Labs**: Practical labs that allow learners to implement these techniques from scratch, ensuring a deep understanding of the process.

**Coverage of Tasks**
The course explores how to apply these techniques in different scenarios:
- **Customer Service**: Ensuring agents can provide accurate and timely responses.
- **Coding**: Helping agents understand and execute complex coding tasks.
- **Research**: Enabling agents to perform thorough and relevant research.
- **Email Management**: Teaching agents to handle and respond to emails effectively.

**Implications for the Future**
