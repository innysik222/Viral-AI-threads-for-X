# President Zelenskyy: The Unyielding Truth About Ukraine's War

**TL;DR:** President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, in a harrowing interview, details the horrors of the war, the Russian invasion, and the determination of his people to achieve a just peace.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**The Horrors of BHA and Beyond**:
President Zelenskyy recounts the unimaginable horrors of BHA, where he found bodies "tied, tortured, and buried in pits." The discovery of a mass grave with 500 tortured people, including children, has hardened the resolve of the Ukrainian people and the international community.

**Russian Miscalculation and Ukrainian Resolve**:
The Russian invasion of Ukraine on February 24, 2022, was a massive military operation with 64 km of armored vehicles heading towards Kiev. Initially, Ukraine's army was only 200,000 strong, but the people's response was overwhelming, with volunteers swelling the ranks to 800,000. The Russian expectation that the people would flee proved to be a miscalculation, as Zelenskyy's refusal to abandon Kiev and the Ukrainian army's fierce resistance led to the failure of the Russian advance.

**Leadership and Determination**:
President Zelenskyy's leadership during the war is characterized by his refusal to abandon the capital and his constant communication with the Ukrainian people. His statement, "I need ammunition, not a ride," to Biden and his family's safety during the invasion illustrate his commitment to his people. Zelenskyy's determination to remain in the office and address the nation in person, even as the situation deteriorated, showcased his unyielding resolve.

**Justice Over Forgiveness**:
The president's stance on justice and accountability is clear. He emphasizes that "you can't just forgive everything like that," referring to the atrocities committed by the Russians. Zelenskyy believes that while a ceasefire is necessary, justice must be served, and the guilty must be punished. The path to peace, he argues, must include a commitment to justice and accountability.

**Impact on Families and the Future**:
The war is irrevocably changing the lives of Zelenskyy's family and the people of Ukraine. Zelenskyy's daughter has grown up during the conflict, and the war has left indelible marks on both his personal and professional life. The president's emphasis on the need for peace and justice underscores the hope for a better future for Ukraine and its people.

### 💬 Notable Quotes

### 🏁 Conclusion
President Zelenskyy's harrowing account of the war paints a picture of a nation's resilience and determination in the face of aggression. The need for justice and accountability, alongside a path to peace, will be central in the coming years as Ukraine seeks to heal and rebuild.
