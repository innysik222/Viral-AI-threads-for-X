## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# AI's Dystopian Future: A Closer Look at Dellisted

**TL;DR:** Dellisted, a new film, warns about the dangers of AI as a tool for government control, impacting jobs, and eroding human connections. The film highlights how AI could lead to a surveillance state and the dehumanization of society.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Government Control:** The film illustrates how AI could be used by governments to suppress dissent and control the masses.
*   **Impact on Jobs:** Dellisted shows how AI could lead to job displacement, impacting livelihoods and social harmony.
*   **Humanity's Loss:** The film emphasizes the importance of human connection and the potential for AI to erode our sense of self and humanity.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Government Control and AI Surveillance**
The film Dellisted paints a bleak picture of a future where AI is used as a tool for government control. Director Michael Clarkson explains, "The moment I started writing this, I thought the most important thing was to humanize the issue and show the people impacted. This is going to happen to them, and if it's going to happen to them, it's going to happen to you." (Clarkson, 2026)

**Impact on Jobs and Social Harmony**
Dellisted also delves into the impact of AI on jobs and society. The film shows a future where driverless trucks and AI-driven education lead to job displacement and a lack of human interaction. "When we're in groups like that and we understand how society works and working together and being part of a collective, that's going to go," says Clarkson. "How are they going to leave school and become interactive with people around them? They become more reliant on technology. They become more enslaved to it." (Clarkson, 2026)

**Humanity's Loss and the Future of AI**
The film also explores the loss of humanity in the face of AI. "To become more human than ever is to remember the power of what humans have," states one of the characters. "We have the power to shape the world, create the world, and agree on the idea of the world. We need to be more human than ever and fight this back." (Clarkson, 2026)

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The AI comes up with this concept of D-listing people and he it makes him very uncomfortable, but he it makes him very uncomfortable, but he sort of as I see it, he hands off decision making to the AI. Why does he do that?" (Clarkson, 2026)

### 🏁 Conclusion
The film Dellisted serves as a stark warning about the potential dangers of AI. It highlights the need for a balanced approach to technology that prioritizes human values and connections. As AI continues to evolve, it's crucial to consider its impact on society and ensure it benefits all.
