## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Can AI Cure All Disease? — Pushmeet Kohli, Google DeepMind & Sal Candido, Biohub (2026-10-10)

**TL;DR:** The conversation on protein folding and AI's role in medicine highlights the need for a collaborative and flexible approach to data and modeling, emphasizing that while significant advancements have been made, the true challenges lie in understanding protein dynamics and design.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Collaboration is Key:** Both modelers and data collectors must work together and be flexible to advance translational medicine.
*   **Data Quality Matters:** While data is crucial, the right data and expertise are essential for meaningful progress.
*   **Understanding the Problem:** Scientists must first define the problem to determine the best solution space, whether it be modeling, data generation, or both.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Collaborative Approach:**
The discussion underscores the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration. Sal Candido emphasized that modelers and data generation experts should not approach the problem with a fixed mindset. Instead, they should be flexible and consider both modeling and data collection as necessary tools. This collaborative approach can lead to better outcomes and a more holistic understanding of complex biological systems.

**Data Quality and Expertise:**
Pushmeet Kohli highlighted that curation of good data is as important as the amount of data. He stated, "It's not just about big data, it's about good data and actually understanding the coverage of what data is necessary for making progress in the problem." This insight stresses the importance of quality over quantity in data collection and highlights the need for domain expertise to ensure that the data is meaningful and useful.

**Understanding the Problem:**
Both experts agreed that defining the problem is critical before diving into modeling or data generation. Sal Candido suggested, "Think of yourself as a multi-disciplinary person. Understand the problem first. Why are you working on this problem? What are we trying to achieve?" This approach ensures that the research is focused and aligned with the ultimate goal of advancing science.

**Future of Medicine:**
The conversation also touched on the potential for AI in accelerating drug discovery. While AI tools like AlphaFold 2 have made significant strides, there is still much work to be done. Pushmeet Kohli emphasized that the true challenges lie in understanding protein dynamics and design, rather than just predicting protein structures.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Sometimes you need to take a step back & ask, 'What is the problem we're trying to solve?' before you dive into modeling or data generation." - Sal Candido

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of medicine is being shaped by AI and collaborative research, but significant challenges remain. By focusing on understanding the problem, ensuring data quality, and fostering interdisciplinary collaboration, we can make meaningful progress in curing diseases and improving human health.
