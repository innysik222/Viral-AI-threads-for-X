# Why AI Compute is Stuck in Rectangles: Erik Bernhardsson, Modal's CEO, Reveals the Future

**TL;DR:** Erik Bernhardsson, CEO of Modal, explains how the company's rapid growth and innovative infrastructure are redefining AI compute, pushing it from rectangles to more flexible, scalable models.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Modal's Rapid Growth:** Revenue has grown 50x in just 2 years, driven by innovative infrastructure.
*   **Sandboxes vs Rectangles:** Modal is reimagining AI compute to be more ephemeral and flexible, moving beyond traditional rectangular models.
*   **GPU Scarcity and Pricing:** The CEO highlights the challenges of GPU availability and long-term pricing, emphasizing the importance of dynamic capacity management.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
Erik Bernhardsson, CEO of Modal, provides a comprehensive overview of the company's journey and future direction in the AI infrastructure space. At the heart of Modal's success is its innovative infrastructure, which has enabled rapid growth and transformed how AI applications are deployed.

#### **Sandboxes vs Rectangles**
Modal's vision for the future of AI compute involves moving away from the traditional rectangular models of GPU allocation. Instead, the company is focusing on ephemeral workloads and more flexible, scalable solutions. According to Bernhardsson, "The world needs better building blocks for AI applications. We're aggregating the world's compute capacity and slicing it out in a very granular manner."

#### **GPU Scarcity and Pricing**
Bernhardsson addresses the challenges of GPU scarcity and long-term pricing. He notes, "The biggest downside of GPU scarcity is not that it's hard to get GPUs, but that almost all capacity is sold as three-year rectangles starting 3 to 6 months in the future. That's not what demand looks like for most users." The company is working on dynamic capacity management to better align with customer needs.

#### **Training and Inference**
Erik is bullish on training, bass jobs, and agent use cases. He explains, "We're building general-purpose building blocks and putting them in front of customers. The customers will combine those building blocks to build incredible applications that we could never have anticipated." This strategy is driving the company's growth in areas like training, reinforcement learning, and low-latency applications.
