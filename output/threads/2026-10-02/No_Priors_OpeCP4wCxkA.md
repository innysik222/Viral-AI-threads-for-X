## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# The Future of Frontier Model Architectures: Insights from Walter Goodwin, Founder & CEO of Fractile

**TL;DR:** Walter Goodwin of Fractile discusses the future of AI chip architectures, emphasizing the importance of speed and memory bandwidth, while highlighting the challenges and opportunities in the chip design process.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Speed and Memory Bandwidth:** Fractile focuses on developing chips with extremely high memory bandwidth to run large models faster.
*   **Full Stack Approach:** The company aims to own the entire chip design process, from workload understanding to physical design.
*   **Speed Chasing:** The company is exploring the scaling laws for bandwidth to reduce the number of flops required for certain levels of intelligence.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

**Speed and Memory Bandwidth:**
Walter Goodwin, founder and CEO of Fractile, highlights the critical role of speed and memory bandwidth in the future of AI chip architectures. He emphasizes that while the industry is moving towards faster models, the challenge lies in maintaining the necessary memory bandwidth to support these models. Fractile's approach is to build chips that can handle thousands of tokens per second, which is essential for applications like snappier chatbots and long-running agents.

**Full Stack Approach:**
Fractile's full stack approach is a significant aspect of their strategy. Unlike traditional chip companies that often outsource parts of the design process, Fractile aims to have complete control over the entire chip design cycle. This includes deep understanding of the target workloads, front-end design, physical design, and backend implementation. Goodwin explains that this full stack approach allows the company to be more agile and responsive to the rapidly changing demands of AI workloads.

**Speed Chasing:**
Goodwin also discusses the challenges of chasing speed in the AI chip space. While there is a clear desire to shorten the latency between observing a new workload and deploying it, there are physical and financial constraints that limit this process. The company is exploring ways to prototype and iterate faster, using AI to automate parts of the design process. However, Goodwin notes that there is still a significant amount of work that cannot be automated, particularly the final check phases that ensure the chip meets the design rules set by foundries.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "If you crack open an Nvidia system, it has anywhere between kind of six and nine custom chips all built by Nvidia to come together to build something that is really, really potent."  
> — Walter Goodwin, Founder & CEO of Fractile

> "You will want something else again in 6 months time like these workloads move on very fast."  
> — Walter Goodwin, Founder & CEO of Fractile

### 🏁 Conclusion
Fractile's strategy of focusing on speed and memory bandwidth, coupled with their full stack approach, positions them to address the rapidly evolving needs of AI workloads. However, the company acknowledges the physical and financial constraints that still limit the pace of innovation. As the industry continues to advance, companies like Fractile will play a critical role in driving the evolution of AI chip architectures.
