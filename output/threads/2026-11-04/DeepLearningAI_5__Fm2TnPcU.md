# Mastering the Art of Evaluating AI Agents: A New Course from Arize AI and deeplearning.ai

**TL;DR:** A new course from Arize AI and deeplearning.ai teaches a systematic approach to evaluating and improving agentic applications, taking the guesswork out of AI engineering.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   Evaluations are crucial in AI engineering to systematically identify and fix issues in agentic applications.
*   The course provides hands-on labs to create test cases and build code evaluators.
*   Reliability of LLM judges is essential for tracking progress and ensuring the agent is improving.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### **The New Challenge of AI Engineering**
Traditional software development has its problems, but AI agentic applications bring new complexities. Agents can make up information, call the wrong tools, or skip required steps. These issues can be challenging to diagnose and fix, leaving developers in the dark about what needs to be improved. This is where the new course from Arize AI and deeplearning.ai comes in.

#### **Systematic Evaluation and Improvement**
The course teaches a structured approach to evaluating agentic applications. It starts by analyzing failures in the agent's traces, turning them into test cases. These test cases can then be rerun after every change to ensure improvements are being made. The course also covers building code evaluators and LLM judges to catch those failures and assess their reliability. This process helps developers make informed decisions on what to improve and verify that the agent is indeed getting better.

#### **The Importance of Reliability**
One of the key insights from the course is the importance of reliability in LLM judges. These judges are used to catch failures and ensure that the agent is improving over time. Without reliable judges, the evaluations may not be accurate, leading to ineffective improvements. The course emphasizes the need to build and test these judges rigorously to ensure their effectiveness.
