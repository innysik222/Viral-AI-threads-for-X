## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Reimagining the Brain as a Computer: Max Hodak’s Visionary Journey with Science

**TL;DR:** Max Hodak, founder of Science (formerly Neuralink), discusses his mission to use advanced neural interfaces to restore vision and ultimately transform human experience by 20 years.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Reframing the Brain as a Computer**: Hodak argues that the brain can be understood and manipulated like any other computer.
*   **Prima Retinal Prosthesis**: The company’s device has successfully restored partial vision to blind patients, marking a significant milestone in neurotechnology.
*   **BCI Landscape**: Hodak distinguishes between different types of BCI products and highlights their potential impact on medicine.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Understanding the Brain as a Computer
Max Hodak believes that understanding the brain as a computer can unlock new possibilities for treating neurological conditions. "The brain very literally, very clearly is a computer," he states, emphasizing its computational nature. This perspective allows for more straightforward and effective approaches to medical interventions.

#### Prima Retinal Prosthesis: A Milestone in Neurotechnology
Science's retinal prosthesis, called Prima, has successfully restored some form of vision to blind patients through advanced gene therapy and electrical stimulation techniques. "Nobody had previously been able to restore a form vision image in the mind’s eye of a blind patient," Hodak notes. While the current version offers only partial visual recovery, it sets the stage for future advancements.

#### BCI Landscape: Beyond Motor Decoding
Hodak describes the broader landscape of BCIs, noting that they range from silent speech devices to advanced neural interfaces. He emphasizes the potential of BCIs beyond motor decoding and their transformative impact on medicine, highlighting areas like vision restoration and substrate independence.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "If you can get the visual signal, auditory signal, balance, motor in and out of the brain, that is an end in itself." — Max Hodak

### 🏁 Conclusion
Max Hodak’s vision of Science is not just about restoring sight but reimagining human experience. By understanding the brain as a computer, they are paving the way for significant advancements in medicine and technology. As Hodak envisions, the future could see us upgrading and replacing parts of ourselves to achieve better healthspan and quality of life.
