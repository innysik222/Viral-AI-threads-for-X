# Legora's Path to $100M ARR: How an Underdog Startup Took on the Legal Tech Industry

**TL;DR:** Legora, a Swedish legal AI startup, has grown to a $100M ARR business by leveraging advanced AI models, customizing workflows, and hiring a dedicated team of forward-deployed engineers. The company has transformed legal work with tools like Tabular Review, which streamlines due diligence, and has a strategic plan to dominate the legal tech market.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Advanced AI Models:** Legora uses a combination of open-source models for specific tasks, fine-tuning them for legal research.
*   **Custom Workflows:** The company has developed a suite of tools like Tabular Review and Intake to streamline legal workflows.
*   **Forward Deployed Engineers:** Legora deploys engineers to client sites to build custom integrations and improve product usage.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Advanced AI Models:**
Legora recognizes that not all legal tasks require Mythos-level intelligence. They use a mix of open-source models like Gemini and Whisper for transcription, while fine-tuning models like Astra for legal research. This approach balances cost and efficiency with the need for high-quality output.

**Custom Workflows:**
The company has developed a range of tools to streamline legal work. For example, **Tabular Review** allows lawyers to review large volumes of documents efficiently. They also use **Intake** to manage client requests and task assignments, making the legal workflow more collaborative and efficient.

**Forward Deployed Engineers:**
Legora deploys engineers to client sites to customize the product and build new integrations. This approach allows the company to provide tailored solutions and improve client satisfaction. The engineers work closely with legal teams to understand their needs and build custom features that fit their workflows.

### 🏁 Conclusion
Legora's success in the legal tech market is a testament to the power of advanced AI and customized solutions. As the legal industry continues to evolve, companies like Legora are poised to dominate by providing tailored tools and workflows that meet the unique needs of legal professionals. The future of legal work is AI-driven, and Legora is leading the charge.
