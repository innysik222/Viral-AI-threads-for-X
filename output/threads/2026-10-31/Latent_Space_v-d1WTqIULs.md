# Unlocking the Future of Financial Services with ChatGPT

**TL;DR:** Joseph Kim, OpenAI's Head of Financial Services, reveals plans for an enterprise-focused ChatGPT product and discusses the challenges and future prospects of financial AI.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Enterprise-Specific Product**
Joseph Kim, OpenAI's Head of Financial Services, presented a new enterprise-focused product, ChatGPT for financial services. The product includes firm-specific templates and sources like Diluta and S&P, making it highly relevant for financial institutions. Kim emphasized the reception was incredible, indicating strong market demand.

**Data Integration**
One of the significant challenges Kim highlighted was the importance of data. He noted that initial attempts to bundle data from third-party providers (MCPs) resulted in high error rates. To address this, OpenAI decided to bring the data in-house, ensuring end-to-end control and higher accuracy. Kim stated, "The error rates were really really high. Some some of the MCPs that we connected some big names 40% plus error rate on their MCPs, right?" This approach ensures the data is reliable and relevant for financial services.

**Industry-Specific Challenges**
Building a financial-specific product requires understanding the industry's reality. Kim explained that product development must consider the competitive forces, objectives, and specific needs of the industry. He highlighted that while the initial product capabilities were impressive, 95% of the work could be done by a startup or another company. The remaining 5% required deeper industry knowledge and specific industry data, making the product more complex and tailored.
