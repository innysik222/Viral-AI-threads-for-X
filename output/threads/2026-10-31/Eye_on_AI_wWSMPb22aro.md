# AI Agents Will Soon Dominate 50% of Your Traffic: How to Prepare Your Data Layer

**TL;DR:** With 50% of traffic expected to be agentic by mid-2027, businesses must prepare their data layers to handle real-time data and optimize for AI agents.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **50% of Traffic Will Be Agentic by 2027:** According to Tealium CEO Jeff Lunsford, 50% of traffic will be generated by AI agents, up from 20% now.
*   **Higher Conversions:** Agentic traffic converts 38% more revenue per session than human traffic.
*   **Real-Time Data is Key:** Real-time data beats batch processing by 30-200% in conversion rates.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### **AI Agents and Human Traffic**
AI agents are set to become a major component of online traffic. According to Jeff Lunsford, CEO of Tealium, 50% of traffic will be agentic by mid-2027. This is a significant shift from the current 20% of inbound traffic being agentic. Lunsford notes, "Even before AI, 40% of traffic was bots. What used to be human traffic is now agentic traffic."

#### **Higher Conversions and Real-Time Data**
Real-time data is crucial for higher conversion rates. Lunsford explains, "Real-time experiences convert at 30 to 200% higher rates than delayed experiences." This highlights the importance of having a dynamic data layer that can handle real-time data and optimize for AI agents. 

#### **Data Layer Optimization**
Tealium's data orchestration layer helps businesses prepare for agentic traffic. The platform collects, enriches, and distributes data in real time, providing trusted and current context to AI agents. Lunsford emphasizes, "We're just helping our customers optimize their data layer. Agents like JSON, JSON-LD, which is basically kind of a pre-configured definition of what data elements mean."

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Real-time data is a better way. Your conversions will get better. Your support costs will be lower. Your processing costs will be lower. Your storage costs will be lower." - Jeff Lunsford, CEO of Tealium

As AI agents become more prevalent, businesses must prepare their data layers for real-time data and optimize for AI-driven traffic.
