## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Liquid A's Ramin Hasani on Building Smarter, More Dynamic AI

**TL;DR:** Liquid A's Ramin Hasani discusses the evolution of AI from static models to continuously learning, adaptive systems, emphasizing the importance of real-time learning and efficient on-device deployment.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Dynamic Learning:** Static evaluations become obsolete as models must adapt in production.
*   **Silicon-Aware Architecture:** New models are designed to fit next-gen chips, improving efficiency and performance.
*   **Multimodal Intelligence:** LFM2 and LFM2.5 models process and output audio, video, and text, revolutionizing physical-world interactions.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Real-Time Learning and Continuous Improvement
Ramin Hasani, CEO of Liquid A, highlights the shift from static models to those capable of continuous learning. "Static evals become obsolete as models evolve in production," he says. This means that AI systems must adapt to new data and contexts in real-time, ensuring that they remain relevant and effective.

#### Silicon-Aware Architecture
Hasani emphasizes the importance of designing models that are optimized for specific hardware. "We're building models that fit perfectly on next-gen chips like Qualcomm's," he explains. This approach ensures that AI systems can run efficiently on devices with limited computational resources, making them more practical for real-world applications.

#### Multimodal Intelligence
Multimodal models, such as LFM2 and LFM2.5, can process and output a variety of data types, including audio, video, and text. "These models can revolutionize how AI interacts with the physical world," Hasani notes. This capability is particularly important for applications like robotics, where AI must interpret and respond to multiple types of sensory inputs.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "We're building models that fit perfectly on next-gen chips like Qualcomm's." - Ramin Hasani, Liquid A

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of AI lies in systems that can adapt and learn continuously. Liquid A's approach, with its focus on real-time learning, silicon-aware architecture, and multimodal capabilities, is a step towards smarter, more dynamic AI ecosystems. As AI becomes more integrated into our daily lives, the ability to adapt and learn in real-time will be crucial.
