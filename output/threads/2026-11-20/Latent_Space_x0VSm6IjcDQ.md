## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Scaling Linear's GTM to $100M+ ARR - Insights from COO Cristina Cordova

**TL;DR:** Linear COO Cristina Cordova shares how the company scaled from a self-serve model to enterprise sales, and why "issue tracking is dead."

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   Linear transformed from a self-serve product to a robust enterprise solution.
*   The company pivoted to focus on agentic workflows and automation.
*   Maintaining a flat organizational structure and avoiding a sales team until necessary helped Linear stay agile.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Scaling Strategy:**
Cristina Cordova, COO of Linear, details the company's journey from a self-serve product to a robust enterprise solution. Linear initially focused on a product-led growth model, but as they grew, they realized the need to sell to larger companies. Cordova explains that Linear didn't immediately hire a sales team, preferring to remain lean and agile until they reached a stage where selling was necessary. This approach allowed Linear to stay close to their customers and understand their needs better.

**Agentic Workflows:**
A key shift in Linear's strategy was the focus on agentic workflows. Cordova explains, "The workflow that I talked about with like you know this like manual intervention by humans at every step of the process is not going to be the work that we live in." Linear recognized the need to build a system that could handle more automation and reduce manual intervention. This new approach not only made the tool more efficient but also aligned better with the changing landscape of product development.

**Customer Experience:**
Cordova emphasizes the importance of maintaining a high level of customer experience. "We have internal folks who work on support, too," she notes, highlighting Linear's commitment to not outsource support. This approach helps Linear maintain a high-quality customer experience, which is crucial for both existing and new customers. Cordova also shares that Linear avoids using AI for customer support, preferring human quality checks to ensure the best customer experience.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The great thing about our business is that we're not like so big to like kill ourselves, right? Like [laughter] it's probably really hard if you're Google to like kill the search business, right?"

### 🏁 Conclusion
Cristina Cordova's insights provide valuable lessons for other startups and established businesses looking to scale. Her advice to stay close to customers, focus on what's changing, and maintain a lean structure until necessary can be applied across various industries.
