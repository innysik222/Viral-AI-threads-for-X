## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Why Humans Can Form Shared Intentions, But Chimps Can't

**TL;DR:** Humans can form shared intentions and create culture, while chimps cannot. This unique ability may help us avoid global catastrophes, but also leads to complex social issues.

### 🔑 Key Insights
- **Shared Intentionality:** Humans can form shared goals and roles within a group, which is unique compared to other species.
- **Culture and Language:** These are emergent phenomena that arise from shared human experiences, not just individual decisions.
- **Agency Levels:** Humans have four levels of agency, while other primates only have basic and goal-directed agency.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### The Evolution of Agency
Michael Tomasello discusses the differences between human and chimpanzee agency, emphasizing that humans can form shared intentions and roles. This is a significant evolutionary step that sets us apart from other great apes. Tomasello argues that shared intentionality is a key factor in human cooperation and the formation of culture.

#### Shared Agency and Collective Intentionality
Tomasello highlights that shared agency and collective intentionality are not just social constructs but have real-world implications. He explains that humans can collaborate and create shared goals, whereas chimps do not correct each other when one does not play their role correctly. This ability is crucial for human cooperation and social organization.

#### The Role of Culture and Language
Tomasello emphasizes that culture and language are not just learned but are also created collectively. He suggests that we are born into cultures, which are collective agencies with conventions, norms, and institutions. This collective agency is what gives humans the unique ability to create and use language, as seen in children who create novel gestures and conventionalize them.

#### The Future of AI
Tomasello also touches on the future of artificial intelligence, suggesting that for AI to be truly agentic, it must start with shared agency and shared intentionality. This means that AI must learn from shared human experiences, rather than just mimicking individual decisions.

#### Notable Quotes
> "Shared agency and shared intentionality are the sort of infrastructure of the creation and use of linguistic conventions." - Michael Tomasello

### 💬 Conclusion
Tomasello's work highlights the unique capabilities of human agency and the critical role it plays in our social and cultural development. As we continue to push the boundaries of AI, understanding these principles will be crucial for creating more agentic and cooperative systems.
