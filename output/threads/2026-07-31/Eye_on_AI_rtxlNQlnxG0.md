# The True Cost of AI Transformation: Half Your Salary Over Two Years

**TL;DR:** The CEO of Liatrio, Chris Blackburn, reveals that transforming an organization into modern ways of working with AI will cost half everyone's salary over two years. This transformation is not just about efficiency but about enabling a new future workforce.

### 🔑 Key Insights
- **Cost of Transformation**: Half the average employee’s salary over two years.
- **AI’s Impact on Jobs and Life**: AI will change jobs, which in turn will change life itself.
- **Leadership Engagement**: CEOs need to walk their factory floors (or digital ones) to understand organizational challenges.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Cost of Transformation
Chris Blackburn, founder and CEO of Liatrio, asserts that the true cost of transforming an organization into modern ways of working with AI is substantial. He states, "You are likely going to spend the equivalent of everyone's salary over a two-year period." This means if the average employee earns $150,000 per year, the total cost for half their salary over two years would be approximately $150 million for a 780-person team. These numbers are "absolutely insane," and CFOs will have to play a significant role in calculating ROI.

#### AI's Impact on Jobs and Life
Blackburn is optimistic about the future, believing that AI will not just change jobs but life itself. He states, "I believe that things are going to move faster than they actually do. So, there's going to be a time horizon to this and all of that. But I just believe so deeply that it's technology and AI is just the technology of choice today." Blackburn envisions an AI-driven future where people can extend their health span, have shorter work weeks, and pursue passion projects.

#### Leadership Engagement
Leadership engagement is critical for successful transformation. Blackburn advocates for CEOs to walk their factory floors or digital ones to understand the operational challenges first-hand. He emphasizes that leaders need to be involved in this process: "I just don't think there's enough of a commitment towards the transformation. I don't think there's enough understanding of what it truly means to change." Leaders must also encourage walking the shop floor and using tools like value stream mapping to identify bottlenecks.

#### Strangler Fig Pattern
One innovative approach Blackburn suggests is the "strangler fig pattern," where a new division or practice is built inside an organization with minimal restrictions. This allows people to act freely and operate in a modern way, enabling them to adapt quickly to new technologies and processes.

The transformation journey with AI will require significant financial commitment and leadership engagement.
