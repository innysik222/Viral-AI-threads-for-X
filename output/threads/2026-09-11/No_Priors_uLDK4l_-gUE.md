# The Future of Finance: AI, Stablecoins, and Agentic Finance with Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong

**TL;DR:** Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong predicts the rise of the Agentic Economy, where AI agents will have their own financial accounts and play a significant role in commerce and finance.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Agentic Finance**: AI agents will have their own financial accounts, enabling them to make payments and perform trading.
*   **Stablecoins**: These are revolutionizing global payments, with over 76% of Agentic Commerce transactions under 30 cents.
*   **Prediction Markets**: They are growing rapidly and could inform government policies and other societal decisions.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### **The Rise of the Agentic Economy**
Brian Armstrong envisions a future where AI agents outnumber humans in the near future. Coinbase is developing tools to bank these AI agents, ensuring they have their own financial accounts and can participate in the global economy. These accounts will be self-custodial and use stablecoins, allowing for fast and cheap transactions.

#### **Stablecoins and Global Payments**
Armstrong points out that stablecoins are becoming the backbone of global payments. With 76% of Agentic Commerce transactions under 30 cents, traditional payment methods like credit cards are not cost-effective. Stablecoins, on the other hand, offer quick and cheap transactions, making them ideal for small-scale commerce.

#### **Prediction Markets**
Armstrong believes prediction markets are growing fast and could have broader implications beyond entertainment. These markets could inform government policies, predict economic outcomes, and even address fundamental questions about the nature of existence.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "We're helping companies all over the world integrate stablecoin payments which are fast and cheap and global. You can send money at the speed of information, right? It's kind of like under 1 second, under one cent US to move money anywhere in the world."

### 🏁 Conclusion
The future of finance is being reshaped by AI, stablecoins, and the rise of the Agentic Economy. As AI agents become more prevalent, they will play a significant role in commerce and finance. Following developments in this space will be crucial for staying ahead of the curve.
