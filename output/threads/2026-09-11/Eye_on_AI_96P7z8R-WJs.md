## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# Quantum Leap: The Future of Cybersecurity with Qanapi

**TL;DR:** Qanapi's innovative approach to cybersecurity, based on assuming data will always be exposed, promises a new era of security without compromising usability.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Assume Data Exposure:** Qanapi starts from the premise that data will always be exposed and focuses on protecting it at the data level.
*   **Granular Encryption:** Data is encrypted at the individual field level, ensuring that sensitive parts are protected while allowing the rest to remain accessible.
*   **Identity and Policy Management:** Keys are tied to specific identities and conditions, ensuring that only authorized parties can access the data.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Assume Data Exposure**
Trent Telford, CEO of Qanapi, emphasizes that the traditional approach to cybersecurity, which focuses on building walls around data, is ineffective. "The bad guys will always find a way in," he says. "We need to change our mindset and start from the assumption that data will always be exposed."

**Granular Encryption**
Qanapi's solution involves encrypting data at the finest granularity—individual words, paragraphs, or even cells in a database. "We can encrypt individual parts of a file, down to the level of individual words or paragraphs," Telford explains. This ensures that sensitive data is protected without hindering the usability of the rest of the information.

**Identity and Policy Management**
To manage these keys effectively, Qanapi ties them to specific identities and conditions. "It's like giving your house key to a trusted friend," Telford elaborates. "You can control who has access and under what conditions." This approach not only enhances security but also ensures that sensitive data is only accessible to authorized parties.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "We start from a baseline assumption that the data will be exposed. Let me uh give a little recap as I understand the history of of uh cyber security or protecting enterprise systems." - Trent Telford, Qanapi

### 🏁 Conclusion
Qanapi's approach to cybersecurity represents a significant shift in thinking. By assuming data will always be exposed and implementing granular encryption and identity-based key management, Qanapi offers a solution that is both secure and functional. For enterprises, this is not just a new technology; it is a new way of thinking about data security.
