# The Race for AI Efficiency: Why Diffusion Models Will Win the Future

**TL;DR:** In a conversation with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon, we explore why diffusion models will dominate AI inference, especially in text and code generation, due to their inherent speed and efficiency. Ermon shares insights on why speed will define the future of AI and how Inception is working on delivering faster and more efficient models.

### 🔑 Key Insights
* **Speed and Efficiency:** Ermon emphasizes that speed and efficiency will be crucial in the next few years of AI development.
* **Diffusion Models:** These models are inherently more parallel, making them better suited for inference tasks compared to auto-regressive models.
* **Commercial Scale:** Inception is betting on diffusion models for commercial-scale language models, aiming to offer faster and more efficient solutions.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### The Future of AI: Speed and Efficiency

Stefano Ermon, co-founder and CEO of Inception, highlights the importance of speed and efficiency in the future of AI. According to Ermon, "Inference time scaling and efficiency are going to be the name of the game in AI over the next few years." This shift is driven by the need to process data faster and more efficiently, especially as AI applications become more widespread.

#### The Advantages of Diffusion Models

Ermon explains that diffusion models, which are based on iterative denoising, offer a more parallel approach compared to auto-regressive models. "A diffusion model is built to have an inference workload that maps really well to GPUs," he says. This inherent parallelism makes diffusion models more efficient, allowing them to generate text and code much faster than auto-regressive models.

#### Commercial Applications

Inception is leveraging these advancements to develop faster and more efficient models. Ermon states, "Our Mercury models are on par with the speed-optimized models from Frontier Labs while being significantly faster." This means that Inception can deliver models that are not only as effective but also more efficient, making them attractive for a wide range of commercial applications.

#### Open-Ended Opportunities

Ermon emphasizes that the field of AI is still in its early stages, with many opportunities for innovation. "Inception is a great place to be if you want to shape the direction of the field," he says. With fewer established practices and more room for experimentation, Inception is attracting talented researchers and engineers who are eager to explore new frontiers.
