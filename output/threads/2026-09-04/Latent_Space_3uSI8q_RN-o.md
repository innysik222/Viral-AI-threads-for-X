# The Inference Frontier: From 100 to 10,000 Tokens per Second

**TL;DR:** Cerebras CTO Sean Lie discusses the future of ultra-fast inference, highlighting how the CS-5 will revolutionize real-time AI applications, while emphasizing the importance of heterogeneous integration and innovation in memory and interconnect technologies.

### 🔑 Key Insights
- **Ultra-Fast Inference:** Cerebras' CS-5 will deliver 10,000 tokens per second, making real-time, interactive applications possible.
- **Heterogeneous Integration:** Cerebras believes in a heterogeneous disaggregated ecosystem, using different architectures to optimize specific tasks.
- **Strategic Partnerships:** OpenAI's partnership with Cerebras is strategic, balancing internal use and enterprise access.

### 🧠 Deep Dive

#### **Ultra-Fast Inference: The Future of AI**
Sean Lie, CTO of Cerebras, emphasized that the future of AI inference is ultra-fast, with the company's CS-5 promising to run GPT-5 at 5,000 tokens per second. This breakthrough will enable real-time, interactive applications, such as graphic design and interactive model training, where speed and efficiency are crucial.

#### **Heterogeneous Integration: The Key to Innovation**
Cerebras is a strong believer in a heterogeneous disaggregated ecosystem. Lie highlighted that as the scale of deployments increases, the need for tailored hardware solutions becomes more apparent. This includes prefill and decode disaggregation, as well as more specialized hardware for specific tasks. The company sees this as the future of AI, where different architectures can be used to optimize different parts of the workload.

#### **Strategic Partnerships: Balancing Internal Use and Enterprise Access**
Cerebras is strategically leveraging its partnership with OpenAI. Lie explained that while a significant portion of the capacity is currently being used by OpenAI for critical internal applications, the company is also making the ultra-fast inference technology available to enterprise customers for high-value applications. This strategic balance ensures that both the company and its customers can benefit from the latest advancements in AI hardware.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "Not just the fact that you wish to show these really, really big numbers, which is also really cool, um, but but the fact that you can, you know, really start to do things that you can't really do, uh, otherwise and and and start to enable brand new capabilities." - Sean Lie, CTO, Cerebras

### 🏁 Conclusion
Cerebras is at the forefront of the AI inference revolution, pushing the boundaries of what is possible with ultra-fast, ultra-efficient hardware. As the industry continues to evolve, companies like Cerebras will play a crucial role in shaping the future of AI and its applications.
