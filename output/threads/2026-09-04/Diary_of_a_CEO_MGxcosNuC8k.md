*   **Exercise Early:** Working out in the first three hours of the day can significantly boost cortisol and energy levels.
*   **Hydration:** Hydrating first thing in the morning can boost morning cortisol levels and mental sharpness.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Cortisol Levels
Andrew Huberman explains that high morning cortisol is essential for elevated mood, focus, and attention. He emphasizes that low cortisol in the morning and high cortisol in the evening can lead to a shorter lifespan and reduced recovery from illnesses.

#### Sunlight Exposure
Huberman advocates for getting sunlight or 10,000 lux artificial light in the first hour of your day to activate your circadian clock. He stresses the importance of this light exposure for setting your internal clock and reducing stress.

#### Side Sleeping
Side sleeping, particularly on your left side, can improve cognitive function and reduce the risk of dementia. Huberman also notes that sleeping on your side can eliminate bags under the eyes, making you look more refreshed.

#### Exercise Early
Working out in the first three hours of your day can significantly boost cortisol levels, which can help you feel more alert and energized throughout the day. Huberman recommends doing cardiovascular or resistance training to enhance your overall performance.

#### Hydration
Hydration is crucial for mental sharpness and boosting morning cortisol levels. Huberman suggests consuming 16-32 ounces of water first thing in the morning to ensure proper hydration.

### 🏁 Conclusion
Implementing these 10 protocols can have a profound impact on your daily life, enhancing your mental and physical health. By following Huberman's advice, you can improve your morning cortisol, set your circadian rhythm, and boost your overall well-being.
