## 📰 SUMMARY ARTICLE

# 36 Months to Go AI-Native or Get Left Behind: Drew Cukor’s Warning

**TL;DR:** American companies have just three years to fully integrate artificial intelligence (AI) into their operations, according to Drew Cukor, former Marine and JP Morgan AI executive. Failure will result in being overtaken by more tech-savvy competitors.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **36-Month Deadline**: Companies must adopt AI within 36 months or risk falling behind.
*   **AI-Driven Disruption**: Legacy companies face competition from AI-native startups that can innovate and perform faster.
*   **Data Management**: Moving data to platforms where AIs can analyze it in real time is essential.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### The Urgency of Transformation

Drew Cukor, a former Marine intelligence officer and executive at JP Morgan, has issued a stark warning to American companies: adopt artificial intelligence (AI) or risk being left behind. His message resonates with urgency, emphasizing that the time for transformation is limited—companies must make the switch within 36 months.

#### Why AI is Critical

Cukor argues that traditional software tools like PowerPoint and Excel are no longer sufficient in today's tech-driven world. "It’s not fine to fight wars on PowerPoint & Excel anymore," he asserts, highlighting how these tools are inadequate for modern business operations. The solution? Move data into platforms where AIs can analyze it in real time, enabling faster decision-making and more efficient workflows.

#### AI-Driven Disruption

The core of Cukor’s warning is the looming threat from AI-native startups. These companies can innovate and perform faster than legacy firms that remain stuck with outdated technologies. "Companies failing to adopt AI will face competition from AI-native startups," he emphasizes, stressing the importance for businesses to act now.

#### Data Management and Transformation

To achieve this transformation, Cukor outlines several key steps:
- **Securing Data**: Ensure data is properly managed and accessible.
- **Building Use Cases & Workflows**: Integrate AIs into business processes to create new layers of insight.
- **CEO Ownership**: CEOs must take ownership of AI initiatives, rather than delegating them to AI officers.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "It’s not fine to fight wars on PowerPoint & Excel anymore." - Drew Cukor

### 🏁 Conclusion
In summary, the future of American businesses lies in embracing AI as a core component of their operations. Failure to do so will result in being overtaken by more tech-savvy competitors. Companies must act now to transform into AI-native entities or risk falling behind.
