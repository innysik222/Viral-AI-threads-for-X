### 🧠 Deep Dive
**Massive Datasets:**
Leash Bio's approach hinges on generating massive chemical libraries using combinatorial chemistry. This method allows them to screen millions of molecules in a single experiment, vastly expanding the scope of small molecule research. By analyzing the interactions of these molecules with proteins, the company aims to identify potential drug candidates more efficiently than traditional methods.

**Hermes Model:**
The Hermes model, developed by Leash Bio, is a simple architecture that combines evolutionary modeling embeddings for proteins and a token predictor for chemistry. Despite its simplicity, Hermes outperforms more complex models in predicting binding activities. This suggests that the key to effective machine learning in drug discovery might lie in simplicity and efficiency.

**Attention Mechanisms:**
Leash Bio's attention maps reveal that AI models might be more focused on memorizing patterns rather than learning underlying principles. This insight highlights the need for more nuanced approaches to ensure that AI models truly understand the chemistry and biology involved in drug discovery.
