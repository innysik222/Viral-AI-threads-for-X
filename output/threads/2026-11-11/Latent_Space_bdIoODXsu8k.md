### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Naive Approach: A Sign of Strength
Zeno Rocha's journey began with Resend as an indie hacker. His background in front-end development and his lack of knowledge about email made him uniquely positioned to approach the problem from a fresh perspective. "I just look at all the tools around that and I've used all of them before. The mailguns, the male chimps, the sendgrids of the world, and I've always felt like man, there should be something where someone cares about the developer persona," Zeno explained. His naivety allowed him to start with a clean slate, focusing on what developers truly need.

#### Innovation and Focus
Resend's success lies in its commitment to innovation and delivering the best user experience in every programming language. Zeno emphasized the importance of having SDKs for every single language. "We started with the most high-level layer and that served us really well," he said. This focus on user experience and developer convenience has helped Resend stand out in a crowded market.

#### Customer Trust and Deliverability
Deliverability is a critical aspect of email marketing, and Zeno prioritizes it over aggressive growth tactics. "The whole point of Twilio Sangid acquisition is that I now you're no longer an email service. You're a communications platform. It is about reaching you across multiple channels," Zeno noted. His approach is focused on maintaining strong relationships with mailbox providers and building robust systems to ensure emails are delivered.

#### AI Integration
Resend is integrating AI in the CLI and MCP, staying ahead of the latest trends in the industry. "Everyone was saying MCP was dead. Like yeah, the only thing that matters is the CLI. What I've noticed is that I stopped paying attention to what's in, what's out," Zeno said. This focus on staying current with AI trends and integrating them into their platform is a key differentiator for Resend.

#### Company Values
Zeno's personal values of family and meaningful work significantly influence his business decisions. "I would rather live in that reality where I'm with my daughter and she wants to be with me, than a reality where we're a 100 million dollar company and she doesn't want to play with me," he explained. This philosophy reflects in his business decisions, prioritizing user trust and meaningful work over aggressive growth.
