# Chain of Thought Meets DNA: Radical Numerics' Bold Move in Genomics

**TL;DR:** Radical Numerics is revolutionizing genomics with advanced AI models that can both design and predict the function of DNA sequences, raising critical questions about biosecurity.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Multi-modal AI Models:** EVO and Omni are designed to handle various biological sequences, pushing the boundaries of what AI can do in genomics.
*   **Chain of Thought in Genomics:** The concept of "chain of thought" is being applied to DNA, allowing models to predict and design sequences in a more structured manner.
*   **Biosecurity Concerns:** The company emphasizes the need for dual-mandate AI, focusing on both design and defense capabilities.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Multi-modal AI Models
Eric Nguyen, CEO of Radical Numerics, explains how their models, such as EVO and Omni, are designed to handle various biological sequences. "EVO was intriguing to folks in many ways. It showcased the potential for applying to multiple types of modalities, but it still underperformed some specialized models," says Nguyen. Omni, on the other hand, has shown promising results across a wide range of tasks, pushing the frontier of each area.

#### Chain of Thought in Genomics
The application of "chain of thought" in genomics is a novel approach that allows models to predict and design sequences more effectively. "We wanted to bring a lot of that research and more to genomics," says Eric. The models are fine-tuned and aligned to showcase specific tasks, which is crucial for practical use in the field of genomics.

#### Biosecurity Concerns
The dual mandate of design and defense is a critical aspect of Radical Numerics' mission. "Inherently, there is this arms race style dynamic that the defensive side has been far far lagging," says Nguyen. The company emphasizes the importance of creating tools that can detect and characterize potentially dangerous sequences, adding a layer of biosecurity to the rapidly advancing field of AI and genomics.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "What I think broadly what I worry about is we're lowering the bar for how much expertise is needed and the speed at which folks can engineer these kind of things," says Eric.

### 🏁 Conclusion
As AI continues to advance, the potential for both incredible breakthroughs and significant risks becomes increasingly apparent. Radical Numerics' work on multi-modal AI models and the application of chain of thought in genomics is at the forefront of these developments. The company's dual mandate approach is a commendable effort to ensure that the benefits of AI in genomics are balanced with the need for robust biosecurity measures.
