### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Insecure and Unreliable GPU Clouds
Jordan, a technical staff member at Semi Analysis, reveals that 90% of the 323 GPU clouds tested in ClusterMAX 3.0 are insecure and unreliable. This is a significant finding, highlighting the critical need for better security practices in the industry. ClusterMAX 3.0, in its third iteration, is raising the bar by testing the latest GPUs like the GB300 NVL72 for reliability and performance, making it the go-to benchmark for lab owners.

#### Leading Players in Bare Metal GPU Rentals
OpenAI and Anthropic are leading in renting large-scale bare metal GPUs, while Google is lagging behind. OpenAI is a driver of Core's business and has signed a massive deal with Oracle, while Anthropic has a strong partnership with AWS. Google, on the other hand, is falling behind, with their deals with Corewave and SpaceX showing their strategic shifts.

#### SpaceX's Contract with Google
The contract between SpaceX and Google is a new dynamic, allowing SpaceX to pull compute back when needed. This structure is interesting as both companies have mutual 90-day cancellation rights, creating a standoff where both are trying to maintain their competitive edge. This contract also highlights the shifting landscape of cloud computing, with companies like SpaceX considering becoming Neoclouds on an opportunistic basis.

#### Nvidia's Acquisition Strategy
Nvidia is on an acquisition spree, buying up talent like Poolside and Hugging Face to secure its ecosystem. Nvidia's business generates almost $50 billion of free cash flow a quarter, and its corporate strategy team is making investments and acquisitions to stay ahead of its competition. This move is defensive as Nvidia wants to ensure that these companies stay in the Nvidia ecosystem rather than competing against them.
