# The Pro-Human Assembly: A Call to Action Against AI Oligarchs

**TL;DR:** Steve Bannon's Pro-Human Assembly at the 2026 Pro-Human Assembly raises crucial concerns about AI regulation, urging citizens to push back against corporate and government control. Key themes include fear of AI's risks, opposition to Chinese influence, and the need for grassroots-driven regulation.

### 🔑 Key Insights
*   **Grassroots Movement:** The Pro-Human Assembly highlights the power of grassroots efforts, emphasizing the role of everyday citizens in shaping the future of AI.
*   **Regulatory Framework:** Bannon calls for a regulatory framework that prioritizes American interests over those of AI oligarchs and Chinese entities.
*   **Chinese Influence:** Bannon advocates for a complete quarantine of Chinese involvement in AI, citing concerns over technology theft and espionage.

### 🧠 Deep Dive
#### Grassroots vs. Oligarchs
Bannon’s speech underscores the growing distrust in corporate and government institutions, particularly in the context of AI. He argues that the pro-human movement must rise above partisan politics to address the critical issue of AI regulation. Bannon states, "The citizens are not going to be supplicants to the oligarchs anymore. We can't do it. We've seen where that's led us so far." This sentiment reflects a broader movement towards citizen empowerment and direct action.

#### Regulatory Framework
Bannon emphasizes the need for a regulatory framework that protects American interests. He criticizes attempts by AI companies and government entities to socialize risks and liabilities among the American public. Bannon asserts, "The American people are going to drive this as they drove the revolution when the 250th year." This call for citizen-driven regulation highlights the need for a more transparent and inclusive approach to AI development and deployment.

#### Chinese Influence
Bannon’s speech also addresses the role of the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) in AI. He calls for a complete quarantine of the CCP, citing concerns over technology theft and espionage. Bannon states, "No chips. No black marketing chips. No free ride off American technology. No stealing of American tech." This stance reflects a broader concern among pro-human advocates about the global implications of AI and the need to protect national security interests.

### 💬 Notable Quotes
> "The American people are not going to be supplicants to the oligarchs anymore. We can't do it. We've seen where that's led us so far."

### 🏁 Conclusion
Bannon's speech at the Pro-Human Assembly serves as a clarion call for the pro-human movement. By highlighting the need for grassroots-driven regulation, opposing Chinese influence, and pushing back against AI oligarchs, the pro-human advocates aim to ensure that the benefits of AI are shared fairly among all citizens.
