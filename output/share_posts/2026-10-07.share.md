Success in high-stakes industries is often driven by a relentless pursuit of goals and a refusal to accept failure, as argued by Dana White. This "killer instinct" is crucial for building a unique brand and securing a dominant position. However, the film "Delisted" warns that surrendering human decision-making to AI can lead to a "de-humanized" future, where social connections and collective agency are eroded.

How can leaders balance the need for high-risk, innovative decisions with the ethical use of AI to preserve human connection and agency?
