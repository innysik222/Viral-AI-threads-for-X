Spec-driven development (SDD) is transforming how we approach software engineering by allowing developers to define projects through clear, detailed specifications that guide coding agents. This method ensures better maintainability and a more controlled development process, making significant changes with minor updates and maintaining consistent context between sessions.

Why it matters: By defining the project's goals and constraints upfront, SDD ensures that the code produced aligns with the developer's vision, leading to more efficient and maintainable software.
