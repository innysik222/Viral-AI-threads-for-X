AI is fundamentally reshaping our understanding of brain health, with Dr. David Perlmutter emphasizing that lifestyle choices like diet and social connection are the primary determinants in preventing Alzheimer's. This means that simple changes in daily habits could significantly reduce our risk, offering a practical and empowering approach to brain health.

Meanwhile, AI is also transforming drug discovery, as Max Jaderberg of Isomorphic Labs discusses. By using predictive models, AI can reduce the need for extensive and costly real-world experiments, potentially speeding up the development of new treatments. This suggests that AI is not just a tool but a transformative force in healthcare.
