Konstantin Kisin warns of impending civil unrest due to economic stagnation, social instability, and the looming threat of AI. He predicts that if artificial general intelligence surpasses human capabilities, it could treat us as pets or even turn against us. "Would you prefer a Chinese system if you could run your show as a citizen there? Yes," Kisin argues, highlighting China's stability over American freedoms.

Why this matters: Kisin’s warnings about AI and economic instability resonate with current global trends. As AI increasingly influences our lives, how do we ensure it benefits society rather than causing division?
