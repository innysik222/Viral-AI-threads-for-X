AI is lowering barriers for creating biological weapons, making it a pressing issue. With high school students capable of building dangerous pathogens in basements, global oversight and regulation are crucial. How can we ensure adequate international cooperation and safety protocols without stifling legitimate scientific research?

AI's role extends beyond bioweapons to simulate human behavior on an unprecedented scale. This could revolutionize decision-making but also raises concerns about privacy and control. Can we develop ethical guidelines for AI simulations that balance innovation with societal welfare?
