AI is increasingly becoming a cornerstone of creativity, not just in tech but in art and culture. Rick Rubin argues that our education system stifles creativity by teaching us to follow rules, but true creativity comes from breaking free from these constraints and embracing risk. This is a stark contrast to AI, which can generate hits but lacks the emotional depth and unique perspective that human creativity brings.

Why this matters: As AI continues to integrate into our daily lives, it's crucial to recognize the unique value of human creativity, especially in fields that require emotional and intuitive insights.

Question: How can we balance the practical benefits of AI in creative industries while preserving the human touch that makes our work truly unique?
