James Talarico argues that the current economic system, fueled by inequality and crony capitalism, is rigged against the middle class. He warns that AI and automation could exacerbate this issue if not regulated properly, ensuring that the benefits of technological advancement are shared rather than concentrated among the wealthy.

Why this observation matters: Talarico's concerns highlight the need for robust regulatory frameworks to prevent AI and automation from widening the wealth gap. Discussing how we can ensure AI benefits all, not just the elite, is crucial for a fairer future.

Question: How can we design AI and automation policies to benefit the working class and middle class while mitigating job displacement?
