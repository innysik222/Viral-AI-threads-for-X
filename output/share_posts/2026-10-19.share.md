Dying is not the end, but a reunion. Dr. Christopher Kerr’s research reveals that 90% of dying patients experience vivid dreams and visions of loved ones, offering comfort and connection. This challenges the common fear of dying alone and suggests a more peaceful, affirming process than many anticipate.

Why this matters: These findings could profoundly impact how we approach end-of-life care and support, potentially reducing anxiety and improving the quality of life for the dying and their families.
