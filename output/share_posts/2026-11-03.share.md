Legora, a Swedish legal AI startup, has grown to a $100M ARR business by leveraging advanced AI models, customizing workflows, and deploying engineers to client sites. This approach not only streamlines legal processes but also ensures high client satisfaction. By using a mix of open-source models and fine-tuning them for specific tasks, Legora has created a robust suite of tools like Tabular Review and Intake.

Why this matters: As AI continues to integrate more deeply into various industries, Legora’s strategy highlights the importance of customization and direct client engagement. How can other businesses in non-legal sectors apply these principles to enhance their own AI implementations?

Discussion question: How can companies in industries other than legal adopt Legora’s approach of using custom AI models and forward-deployed engineers to improve their operations?
