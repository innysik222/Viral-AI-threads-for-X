AI is no longer just a digital phenomenon; it's transforming the physical world. Bob McGrew of Arda argues that true superintelligence requires advancements in manufacturing and physical infrastructure, not just digital automation. This shift challenges the common belief that automating researchers will lead to runaway AI, highlighting the need for tangible, real-world improvements.

Why this matters: The integration of AI into physical processes could revolutionize industries, but it also raises new ethical and practical questions. How will these changes affect jobs and industry dynamics?

Question: How might the emphasis on physical infrastructure impact the job market in the coming years?
