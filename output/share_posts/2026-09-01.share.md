AI is transforming the American dream, according to David Friedberg, a top White House advisor. He predicts a shift from labor to capital, where 2% of Americans each year will transition to leverage AI's growth, creating new economic opportunities. This shift isn't just about technology; it's about redefining what it means to be financially secure in the 21st century.

Why this matters: As AI continues to integrate into our daily lives, understanding how it shapes economic and social policies is crucial. Will the transition from labor to capital truly democratize wealth, or will it exacerbate existing inequalities?

Question: How can we ensure that AI's benefits are accessible to all, not just the 2%?
