Alex Borez, a key strategist behind the RAISE Act, emphasizes that the real power in passing AI policy lies not just in having a champion, but in understanding the "power mapping" of veto points. This means identifying the groups and individuals who can say "no" to a bill, and strategically organizing a coalition to navigate these obstacles.

Why this matters: As AI continues to integrate into our lives, understanding how to navigate the complex landscape of decision-making will be crucial for ensuring a pro-human approach to AI policy. This insight from Borez highlights the importance of coalition building and strategic planning in shaping the future of AI.
