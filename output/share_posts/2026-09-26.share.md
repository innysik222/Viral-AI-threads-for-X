The Russo-Japanese War of 1904-1905 teaches a critical lesson in military strategy: clear, limited objectives are key to success. Japan's focused goal of expelling Russia from Manuria allowed for a more efficient and successful campaign, while Russia's broad and poorly prioritized objectives led to significant losses. This historical insight is particularly relevant today as AI-assisted decision-making becomes more prevalent in military operations.

How can modern militaries ensure they maintain strategic focus while leveraging AI's speed and efficiency without overextending their resources?
