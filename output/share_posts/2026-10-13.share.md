Eric Weinstein's argument that extraterrestrial programs and the AI race are both being tightly controlled by leadership raises profound questions about the nature of our world. If key entities are hiding truths about both cosmic and technological threats, it suggests that the path to AI superintelligence is fraught with unseen dangers and manipulations. This perspective challenges us to consider whether the pursuit of AI dominance is being driven by genuine scientific progress or by hidden agendas.

How might the existence of "special access programs" involving extraterrestrial entities influence the ethical and safety standards we set for AI development?
